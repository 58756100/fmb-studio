# Ideation

Produce 11 concepts per week: 3 collectors, 3 garages, 3 suppliers, 2 for everyone. Mark 3 of them `lab: true` (one per core audience); those topics also go to lab lanes A, B and C (`lab/README.md`).

0. Read `state/visual-bank.md`. No concept may reuse a premise, story device, main prop or hero car+part pairing already in the bank (Wayne, 2026-10-14: every video must look new). Add each new concept to the bank once it's made.
1. Read `state/learnings.md` first. At least 6 concepts must apply a named learning, and at most 2 may repeat a format that won last week (exploit), while at least 3 try something new (explore).
2. Draw on real tension for each audience (references/audience-tensions.md). Every concept = a character + a moment + a turn where FMB changes the outcome.
3. Assign each concept a format and two to three hook families from `script-producer/references/retention-playbook.md`. Use at least 5 formats per week. Never repeat a hook family back to back on the feed.
4. Mark the product feature each concept relies on, and mark it (confirm) unless it's listed as live in learnings.md.
5. Write `weeks/<week>/brief.md`: one row per concept with id, audience, title, character, moment, turn, format, hook families, feature, and the learning it applies.
