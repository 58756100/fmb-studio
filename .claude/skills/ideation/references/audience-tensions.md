# Audience tensions (seed list; extend with what the comments say)

## Collectors
- History lives in a shoebox, emails and one mechanic's memory. Gaps become a buyer's discount.
- "Any service records?" ends private sales.
- At every sale the car keeps going but its story resets.
- Insurance and appraisal want proof of the work.

## Garages
- Knowledge walks out when a tech leaves.
- "Any update on my car?" calls eat the day.
- Instagram can't tell a great shop from a great photographer.
- Disputes: "you never told me about that" without a record of approvals.
- Non-project shops can graduate to project work with a portfolio.

## Suppliers (parts brands and shops)
- Sales data stops at the shipping label. Installs and outcomes are invisible.
- Sponsored posts peak in a day.
- Reviews are written on day one, but durability shows at 30,000 miles.
- "What turbo is that?" gets answered by whoever replies first, often not the brand.

## Everyone
- One car, three people who each hold a piece of its history.
- The record should outlive the build, the owner and the shop.
