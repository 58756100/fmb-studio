## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Jen, about to hand her green E30 to a stranger for a $9,000 engine swap.
2. **Wants:** the shop that won't wreck her car.
3. **But:** two shops have identical feeds and the identical reply, "We do these all the time." So she asks both: "Show me an E30 you've done." Shop one fires back beauty shots of other cars. Shop two just keeps typing.
4. **Therefore:** shop two sends one link: their E30 swap build page, with one dated job, the car on their lift and their name on the work (FMB shown working, 10.5–16.5 s).
5. **Payoff + loop:** she chose shop two; shop one sits on "Seen". Both feeds snap back to identical, which is where we started, but now you know the difference.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Eric** narrates: crisp, observational, slightly amused. Speed set near 2.7 words/s, never above 3.
- **Jen's question** is read by Eric as a quote, slightly flatter, so it lands as her words. One narrator, no dialogue: the picture's symmetry carries the rhythm.
- **Emphasis words:** Show me an E30 you've done · No E30 · typing · link · their · Seen.
- **Pauses:** short (0.1–0.3 s) everywhere. The longest is the 0.3 s after "Shop two?", which is the stall.
- **VO fit (spoken words / beat length; "E30" read as two words, URL as five):** hook A 4/1.8 = 2.2 · 9/3.2 = 2.8 · 7/3 = 2.3 · 4/2.5 = 1.6 · 7/2.5 = 2.8 · 7/3.5 = 2.0 · 7/3 = 2.3 · CTA 8/3 = 2.7. No beat is over 3 words/s. The tightest is body line 1: if the take runs past 5.1 s, trim the hook break, never the question.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Frame-locked symmetry (0–5.3 s).** Build the left and right phones as one component instanced twice. Every animation and sfx fires on the same frame (doubled, panned L/R) until 5.3 s.
2. **The crack (5.3 s).** The first asymmetric event: the left phone gets photos, the right freezes on "Delivered" with a 1-frame chromatic offset.
3. **The stall (8–10.5 s).** Typing dots on the right run on an irregular rhythm (pulse 0.6 s, stop 0.4 s, pulse). Each left-side photo ping is a little louder than the last.
4. **Whip-pan → collapse (10.5–13 s).** Whip-pan right, then the seam slides off as the build page takes the full frame at 13 s.
5. **One legible entry (13–16.5 s).** The install photo runs at full phone width, the date in Inter Semibold, at least 44 px on the 1080-wide canvas. The attribution chip is neutral grey (#86868B text on #F5F5F7). No check marks or stamps anywhere.
