## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Marcus, selling the white '99 Supra he built over eleven years, to Dale, who is standing in the garage.
2. **Wants:** his asking price, $68,000.
3. **But:** Dale wants proof, and the shoebox is full of faded thermal receipts. Every blank one knocks the offer down, until Dale stops asking anything.
4. **Therefore:** Marcus puts the lid on and hands over his phone: the build page, the turbo entry dated March 12, 2019 with photos of the work, then every other part (FMB shown working, 16–24.5 s).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Dale starts asking again, about the car this time, not the paperwork. The last frame is the shoebox with its lid closed; the first frame is the lid lifting.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Eric** narrates: warm, close-mic, storyteller. Speed set so he lands near 2.7 words/s, slower (~2.3) from 19.5 s.
- **Chris (Dale)** has two lines, rendered separately (`tts.py --dialogue`) with a 150 ms gap. "That the turbo?": flat and skeptical. "Coilovers in '21. Alignment since?": interested, a little faster. The shift between his two reads *is* the payoff, so don't flatten it.

- **Pauses:** the 0.6 s after "Dale went quiet" is the reveal silence, so don't tighten it. Sentence gaps elsewhere 0.15–0.3 s as tagged.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Offer chip (0.1–21 s).** One persistent rolling-number chip in the headline band: "Asking" until 5.5 s, then "Dale's offer". Red only while dropping, grey when dimmed. It never climbs back; at 19.5 s it slides up and out on a soft ease. That exit is the visual for "price stopped being the topic".
2. **Fading receipts (2.5–11.5 s).** Cream cards with thermal type and a visible paper grain, dissolving from the edges in as they near the lens. Soft contact shadow; the coffee ring is the only stain.
3. **Lid-to-phone (14–16 s).** One spring drives both: lid down, phone up, 4-frame stagger.
4. **One legible entry (16–19.5 s).** The turbo entry runs at least 44 px type on the 1080-wide canvas, date in Inter Semibold, photos at full phone width. Scrolling starts only at 19.5 s and moves at readable speed (no flick blur).
5. **Dale's answer (22–24.5 s).** The alignment entry is an iOS sheet that slides up under Marcus's tap, landing on Dale's word "since".
6. **Loop (27–30 s).** Lock box position and scale between the last frame and frame 0. CTA text fades out over 29.6–30 s so the final frame is clean.
7. **Sound.** Garage room tone under the whole body; cardboard, paper and a concrete scuff for tactility. Music out at 11.5 s, back in at 24.5 s.
