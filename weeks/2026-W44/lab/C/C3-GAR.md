# C3-GAR: You never told me

Lab lane C v3, garages. Concept approved by Wayne (weeks/2026-W44/lab/C/v3-concepts.md). Checked against state/visual-bank.md and lab/critiques/2026-W42-chatgpt.md.

- **Audience:** garages
- **Format:** mini story, with dialogue (Okafor and Gus) and a short narrator
- **Runtime:** 27.0 s. Hook 0–1.8 s, body 1.8–24.0 s, CTA over the final story frame 24.0–27.0 s. No end card.
- **Device:** a split-flap board (airport-style, black flaps, white letters) across the top third. It carries the story's words, and every flip clatters. The week fills in row by row, and the Thursday row stays blank: that's the missing call, shown without a line of VO.
- **Learning applied:** a named person and car within 5 s ("Okafor's R34" at 2.0 s, Gus at 3.8 s). Dialogue vs single narrator is the test from learnings.md.
- **Critique principles:** stakes on screen at frame 0 (the red "$1,850" chip and an angry customer at the counter). The core question is asked by 4.9 s. One legible record. Believable payoff: the record shows what was found and when, not that Okafor was told, and Gus owns the missed call. CTA over the story frame, last frame = frame 0.
- **FMB turn:** 12.0 s = 44 % (the entry is legible by 12.6 s). That's under the playbook's 55 % floor, as briefed in v3-concepts.md. Flag for the retention-editor.
- **Characters:** Gus, Mr. Okafor and Pell Street Garage are fictional. "Illustrative story" is on the CTA frame.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Gus, owner of Pell Street Garage, at the counter with Mr. Okafor, who has come for his silver R34.
2. **Wants:** to get paid for an $1,850 head-gasket job and keep a good customer.
3. **But:** Okafor slaps the bell: "You never told me." Gus walks back through the week: car in Monday, found it Tuesday, parts Wednesday, and Thursday... he isn't sure he called. "So you didn't."
4. **Therefore:** Gus turns the counter monitor around. His Tuesday log entry is on the R34's build page: "Oil weep at head gasket", a photo of the weep, logged by Gus at Pell Street Garage (FMB shown working, 12.0–16.0 s). It doesn't prove the call. It proves the problem was real, and when it was found.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "You still should've called." "I should have." The Thursday flap turns to SORRY, and then they talk about the engine. The board clatters back to NEVER TOLD ME and the bell sits ready, which is frame 0.

## 8. Motion notes
1. **The board is built in code.** 18 characters × 4 rows, black flaps with a center seam, about 54 px per flap, white Inter Bold letters at 56 px, across the top 30 % of the frame. Each character flips through 3–8 random letters before it lands, staggered 20 ms left to right. Sfx: one mechanical click per flap, summed into a clatter. It's a word board, not a clock: rows show events, and nothing counts or rolls.
2. **Camera: punch-ins only.** Hard 1-frame punch-ins and punch-outs between the board and the counter. No whip-pans and no push-throughs.
3. **The bell (0.0 s and the loop).** The brass bell cutout gets a 2-frame squash on the ding. Its frame-0 position, scale and shadow match the last frame exactly.
4. **Monitor swing (9.0–12.0 s).** A flat landscape screen built in code (thin black bezel, small stand), seen straight on. It rotates on the Y axis from its dark back to the screen, and there's a soft screen-glow on the counter line. No phone anywhere.
5. **One legible record (12.0–15.0 s).** A desktop build-page layout: one entry, with the photo at about 70 % of the monitor width. It holds still for 3.0 s, with no scroll and no other rows. The attribution chip is #86868B on #F5F5F7. No checks, stamps or circles.
6. **The THU row.** It spins and stalls on blank at 8.5 s, then clatters to "SORRY" at 19.0 s. That's the only flip in that window, so it reads.
7. **Loop (24.0–27.0 s).** Reverse the set dressing back to frame 0: the board on "NEVER TOLD ME", the chip red, the bell in place.

## 10. Bank check (state/visual-bank.md)
- **Premise:** a counter dispute about what was said. Clear of G1 (a record outliving a tech), G3 (update texts), G2/G2-v2/B-L2 (shop choice) and A-L2.
- **Device risk, the closest call:** the board moves through days, and the bank lists "rolling-number counter or clock as the spine" (G1's Fri→Mon clock). To stay clear, the board shows the week as a static list of events filled in row by row: no clock face, no times, no rolling numbers. Its spine is words: NEVER TOLD ME → the week → SORRY → NEVER TOLD ME. I removed the concept's "nine ten" timestamp for the same reason (and because entry times aren't a confirmed live field).
- **Props:** brass bell, flap board and counter monitor, all new. No shop keys (G1), wall clock (G3), sticky note, phone, text thread or mechanic portrait.
- **Product framing:** a counter monitor turned to the customer, not a phone push-through.
- **No circle or stamp on the photo:** G1 and C3 used a scribble circle on a photo. Here the camera eases into the photo instead.
- **Hero pairing:** R34 car-11 (previously grids only) + engine-block (previously text only) is new.
