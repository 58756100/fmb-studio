## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Korvane Turbo, a small turbo brand.
2. **Wants:** to know where its turbos actually end up, on what cars, fitted by whom.
3. **But:** tracking stops at "Delivered", and the only review says "Arrived fast." Neither tells them what car a Korvane turbo is on.
4. **Therefore:** a public build page answers it: a white '99 Supra whose parts registry lists "Turbo kit · Korvane", installed March 14 by Kessler Motorsport, with install photos. Tap the brand and it opens the page we're building to gather every build like it (FMB shown working, 11.5–21 s; the supplier page is labeled concept · in development).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Not just delivered. Installed." The camera pulls back into the turbo kit cutout over its open box, which is frame 0, where the turbo drops in again.
