## VO DIRECTION
One US voice, mid register, conversational with a slight edge of amused frustration. 165–175 wpm during spoken passages. No shouting or announcer delivery. Emphasize the difference between *guess* and *fact*. Pause before "The brand." Shift into a helpful tone at "Here's the fix." Speak the supplier question directly and leave it space.

## CAPTION
"What turbo is that?" deserves more than a confident guess. We put the part and its brand in the build's parts registry, with a public build link to share.
