## VO DIRECTION
One US voice with the easy authority of an experienced service writer. 160–170 wpm during spoken passages. Medium energy; warmer than L1. "You can't tell" is matter-of-fact. Pause briefly after "Same finished car," then land "More to judge." Let the closing question sound like a conversation between people who have done the work.

## CAPTION
The finished-car photo is only part of a shop's story. We help connect the parts, dated photo logs and people behind the work.

Shops: what job looks easy in a photo but takes real skill to get right? Tell us why.

Follow us for better build records. Sign-ups are open at fuelmybuild.com — link in bio/profile.

## RETENTION LOGIC
- Past 3s: a real selection problem: two identical photos and insufficient information.
- Past 5s: "show what the photo leaves out" turns choosing a shop into discovering missing evidence.
- Past 10s: garage and contributor credit reveal the people responsible.
- Past 15s: returning to the identical opening photo makes the difference clear.
- Comment trigger: shops explain difficult, often invisible work; "Tell us why" asks for more than one word.

## FEATURES USED
Build pages with garage and contributor attribution; parts registry with brand per part; public URL; dated log entries; photos.

- Palette: background #22211E; text #F4EBDD; emphasis #E9BB62; panel fill #39362F.
