# Answers to ChatGPT's pre-lane-A questions (pasted into FMB Lab · Blind, 2026-10-14)

1. TOPICS, COUNT, SLOTS
3 videos, one per audience. They post the week of Oct 26 at 12:00 or 19:00 ET
on IG Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts (the same video on all three).
- L1 [collectors] Insurers and appraisers want proof of the work done on a car.
  Most owners can't produce it, so the car is valued as if the work never happened.
- L2 [garages] Small shops that do great work can't show it. Customers judge
  them by a few photos, so they lose the big project jobs to shops that look better online.
- L3 [suppliers] Someone comments "what turbo is that?" under a build, and
  whoever replies first answers it, usually not the brand that made the part.

2. RUNTIME
20-35 s. One master cut for all three platforms; you may write
platform-specific captions and hashtags.

3. AUDIENCE PRIORITY AND ACTION
Equal priority in this test (one each). After the comment, the action is a
follow. A profile visit and the link in bio (fuelmybuild.com) come second.
Don't promise sign-ups, pricing or availability.

4. OFF-LIMITS AND VERIFIED FACTS
Live features you may show: build pages with a public URL; a parts registry
with the brand per part; log entries with dates and photos; garage and
contributor attribution on a build.
Off-limits:
- Any other feature: ownership transfer, client progress pages, supplier
  analytics or maps, payments, AI features.
- Invented statistics presented as real data.
- Fake testimonials, or real people, shops or parts brands named or shown
  without permission (fictional ones are fine).
- Knocking competitors by name.
- Street racing or unsafe driving.
- Politics.
Tone is your call.

5. WHAT CLAUDE CAN DEPICT
- Can:
  - FuelMyBuild app screens, rebuilt as animated mockups
  - kinetic typography and 2D motion graphics (charts, diagrams, UI, transitions)
  - a library of studio cut-out photos to animate: '99 Supra, WRX STI, 240Z,
    Tacoma, NSX, E30, Miata, Civic EG, R34, AE86, RX-7, Evo VI, GR86, Integra,
    Mustang, Corvette; parts: turbo kit, coilovers, wheel, intake, exhaust,
    brake kit; one older-mechanic portrait
  - AI voiceovers (describe the voice you want; two-voice dialogue works)
  - music and SFX built to your spec
- Cannot (yet): live-action filming, stock footage, photoreal AI video.
  Design within that.

Go ahead with lane A for L1-L3 in the agreed format, plus the VISUAL SPEC per
video: look, 2-3 references, palette in hex, typography, shot types, cut
rhythm, per-beat screen layout, and music/SFX with BPM and hit points.
Assume the builder has never seen our brand.
