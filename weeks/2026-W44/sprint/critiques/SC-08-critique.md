# SC-08 — Margin Notes: critique

**Verdict:** A strong feedback-loop story with a real before/after object. The opening is overwritten, the missing communication is overexplained, and the ending turns a fitter's informal fuel-system tip straight into manufacturer instructions without validation. Fix those, not the premise.

Basis: supplied script and quoted standards; `sprint.md` was unavailable. Scores assess lead A and the written cut. Scroll timing is predicted. Remove added headlines/subtitles; the printed sheet and its note are story objects. No technical installation advice is being validated by this critique.

**3 s:** the narrator is still inside a sweeping statement about brands, with the words of the actual note deliberately withheld. Open on Zeke's lost hour and a visible annotation rather than teasing handwriting.

**Hook B — Narrator:** “Zeke's fuel-pump tip kept going in the bin. Corliss never saw it.”

**Hook C — Zeke:** “I wrote in the margin. Next time, it was printed there.” Follow C immediately with “It was a note about fitting this fuel-pump seal.”

## 5. Keep

- Pencil scratch, carbon-blue copy and the visual match between margin and print.
- The owner sending the link: this is how the knowledge actually reaches Corliss.
- Desmond as someone who can act on feedback, not a villain.
- Zeke's quietly pleased final line. Unlike the supplier sales stories, this one changes instructions for the next installer.
