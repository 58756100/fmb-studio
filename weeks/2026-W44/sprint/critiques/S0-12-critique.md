# S0-12 — Thirty-Eight Sets: critique

**Verdict:** This has a real decision and a product-relevant consequence. Its weak point is inference: two owners reporting rubbing do not prove that the wheel diameter caused it or that seventeens solve it. Priya should pause the bulk order and investigate fitment, then make a smaller, informed change.

Basis: supplied script and quoted standards; `sprint.md` was unavailable. Scores cover lead A. Scroll timing is predicted. Remove added headline/subtitle overlays; the order form and necessary owner-record text can remain. No wheel-fitment specification is validated here.
