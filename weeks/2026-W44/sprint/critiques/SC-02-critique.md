# SC-02 — Twenty-Three Parts: critique

**Verdict:** The record catching its own owner is the film. The current version hides the clunk until 31.4 s, repeats its count and lets an advisor diagnose a mechanical fault by reading a page. Rebuild around a useful lead that a tech then checks.

Basis: supplied script and quoted production standards; `sprint.md` was unavailable. Scores cover lead A and the written edit. Scroll timing is a hypothesis. Remove added headline/subtitle overlays under “no captions”; retain only useful object or product text.

## 2. Predicted scroll-away

**8 s:** we are hearing a generic service-counter question after a numerical hook, still without knowing why the Bronco is there. Name the clunk in the hook, then let the inventory become relevant evidence.
