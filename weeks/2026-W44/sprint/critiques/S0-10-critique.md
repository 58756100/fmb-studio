# S0-10 — Photo Credit: critique

**Verdict:** Probably the batch's cleanest short skit. Keep the two voices, photo and “Can my name be bigger?” The fix is conceptual: adding Dani to a car record does not correct the viral post that omitted her. Make Lorraine repair that omission as well.

Basis: supplied script and quoted standards; `sprint.md` was unavailable. Scores assess lead A and the written edit. Scroll timing is predicted. No-caption rule applies to added overlays; the in-story social-post caption is the subject of the dispute, not a required video-caption track.
