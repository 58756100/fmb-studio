# LA-01

**AUDIENCE:** Collectors  
**TITLE:** Bolted In Isn't Finished  
**LENGTH:** 48 seconds. The handover, misleading photo and honest reconstruction need three turns; the final pause lets the distinction land. Hook A plus body is paced at approximately 2.5 words per second, including breaths.

## PREMISE

A collector buys an unfinished coupe with a radiator already mounted and a reassuring claim that the cooling is done.  
The useful discovery isn't another completed job: it's the exact point where the evidence stops.

## VISUAL STYLE

Vertical 9:16; charcoal, faded orange and warm paper. Condensed slab headlines with simple sans-serif labels. An exploded model-kit world made from car photographs and physical paper tabs; curious, then reassuring. Reference: workshop assembly manuals. No live filming or subtitles. All characters, vehicles and examples are fictional; remove real vehicle and parts logos. App views are illustrative layouts of the supplied features, not proposed interface controls.

## CAPTION

“Installed” can mean something very different from “finished.”

An unfinished build deserves a record that says what happened and where the evidence stops. Fictional example.

## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Past 3 seconds:** Collectors recognize an unfinished purchase and a suspiciously broad promise.
- **Past 10 seconds:** The widening photo reveals the missing hoses, giving the opening claim a physical contradiction.
- **To the end:** The payoff is permission to document an unknown honestly; a build page doesn't manufacture certainty.
- **Comment trigger:** Viewers can name a job they thought was complete. The caption question is not an additional spoken or on-screen CTA.
