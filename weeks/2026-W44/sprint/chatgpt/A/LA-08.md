## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Past 3 seconds:** Suppliers recognize the repetitive pre-sale question, with no existing failure or return involved.
- **Past 10 seconds:** The synchronized hood reveal delivers an immediate visual reason that the model year is insufficient.
- **To the end:** The context goes back to the original question while actual fit confirmation remains with the supplier.
- **Comment trigger:** Support staff and builders can name useful surrounding modifications or measurements; no ticket is magically solved by a log.
