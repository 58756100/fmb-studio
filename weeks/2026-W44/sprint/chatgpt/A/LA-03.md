# LA-03

**AUDIENCE:** Collectors  
**TITLE:** Spring You Has Questions  
**LENGTH:** 34 seconds. One memory trap and one reveal carry this idea; a longer version would repeat the joke. Aim for approximately 2.5 spoken words per second, with a short reveal pause.

## PREMISE

A collector opens the garage after winter and sees an empty oil bottle, which seems like reassuring evidence.  
The build log reveals it belonged to a different car; this car's last entry records a drain, with the refill still unrecorded.

Vertical 9:16; cold slate shifts to buttery morning yellow. Rounded sans-serif headlines, tiny calendar numerals. Stop-motion-style photo collage, with a winter cover peeling away like a calendar page. Mood: affectionate self-recognition. Reference: fridge notes and seasonal desk calendars. No live filming, captions or subtitles. All cars and people are fictional; remove real logos. No app reminders or automated maintenance features.

## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Past 3 seconds:** The storage cover and key immediately identify the owner's spring ritual.
- **Past 10 seconds:** The bottle points to the wrong car, overturning a familiar shortcut.
- **To the end:** A specific last-recorded action replaces vague memory; missing documentation remains a question, not proof that a refill never happened.
- **Comment trigger:** Owners recall their own end-of-season loose ends. No retrospective-history tutorial or advance winter-build planning premise.
