# LA-04

**AUDIENCE:** Garages  
**TITLE:** The Shop Behind the Green Door  
**LENGTH:** 44 seconds. The recommendation must travel from a familiar local landmark to somebody outside that circle; approximately 2.5 words per second leaves room for the visual handoff.

## PREMISE

A respected garage survives on recommendations like “the place behind the green door,” which make sense only to locals.  
A customer's public build page gives that recommendation a name, credited work and something concrete to send farther.

## VISUAL STYLE

Vertical 9:16; deep green, cream and hot coral accents. Bold humanist sans-serif, generous negative space. A miniature neighborhood made from photo cut-outs unfolds into a phone-sized build page. Mood: fond of the shop, impatient with the limits. Reference: folded neighborhood maps and independent business cards. No live filming or subtitles. All shops, people, cars and geography are fictional. Remove real logos. No garage profile or directory UI.

## VOICES

One FuelMyBuild narrator, perceived age 35–50, friendly to independent garages. Say “Dock Lantern” like a place you know, without imitating a real owner. Emphasize **doesn't travel**, **credit** and **public link**. No promise of leads, rankings or guaranteed new customers. “We” means FuelMyBuild throughout.

## CAPTION

“The garage behind the green door” only works if you know the green door.

A fictional shop story about helping a recommendation travel with the work attached.

#FuelMyBuild #IndependentGarage #AutoRepair #CarBuilds

**Pinned comment:** What's the local nickname for a shop everybody swears by? Keep real names out of it.

## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Past 3 seconds:** Garage owners see a reputation problem they recognize, represented by one memorable door.
- **Past 10 seconds:** The out-of-town recommendation exposes the limit of a local landmark.
- **To the end:** The same recommendation physically transforms into a customer build page with proper shop and worker credit.
- **Comment trigger:** Local nicknames invite stories without comparing, ranking or criticizing real shops.
