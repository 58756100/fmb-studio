## WHY IT HOLDS

- **Past 3 seconds:** The attractive rebuilt component is placed against the exact doubt a buyer has.
- **Past 10 seconds:** The camera goes under the exterior, turning the vague claim “rebuilt” into a specific sequence of work.
- **To the end:** The source-car connection makes the record credible as context, while the visible gap leaves room for present-day inspection and questions.
- **Comment trigger:** Viewers can specify an image, operation or test they'd ask to see. No mileage claim, warranty promise or lifecycle record beyond the car's page.
