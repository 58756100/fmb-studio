## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Hal, leaving for three weeks, and his daughter Jo, who's keeping his AE86 while he's gone.
2. **Wants:** Jo wants everything he knows about the car before he leaves.
3. **But:** Hal hit the garage button on his way out, his ride is waiting, and every fact he gives is cut short by the door coming down a panel at a time. He's just rambling. Jo seems to be typing, maybe texting.
4. **Therefore:** the door stops on its last line. Jo hasn't been texting. Every fact has been going onto the car's page, and the closed door panels are the record: coolant, idle, timing belt 2019 at Rocco's. FMB is revealed at 20.4 s (55 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the important one: "She's never been out in the rain. Keep it that way." "Logged." The door shuts with the whole record on its face. A knock, and the door rises again, which is frame 0.
