## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Loretta, who owns Brightwater Collision, outside the paint booth while the new front fender for a red '91 Miata bakes.
2. **Wants:** to finish the day with the job written up, so the car's next shop, owner or insurer can see exactly what was done.
3. **But:** she always tells herself she'll write it up tonight, and tonight never happens. Three jobs from last week are still on a clipboard by the booth.
4. **Therefore:** she logs it during the bake. The booth's five ceiling panels go amber one at a time, one per step: the Miata's page, Ozzie's photos, parts and brands, the colour-match note nobody writes down, and the credit. FMB is shown working from 13.6 s (26 %). Because this is a how-to, the steps are the product. The real turn comes at 37.4 s (71 %): the booth is still amber, so last week's three jobs go on too, each with its real date.
5. **Payoff + loop:** the panels cool to white, the door opens, and the clipboard is empty. The door closes again on the next panel, which is frame 0.
