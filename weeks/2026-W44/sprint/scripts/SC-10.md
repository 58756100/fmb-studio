## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Wendell, eleven hundred miles from home in his '67 fastback, on his way to Santa Fe.
2. **Wants:** to get there by Saturday, when his brother gets married and this is the getaway car.
3. **But:** the fastback starts steaming. The next town has one shop, and Leland has never seen the car. There's no fan on the engine, Wendell only knows "it's electric, somewhere", and Leland's options are a day of chasing wires or a mechanical fan that's two days out.
4. **Therefore:** Wendell tells him everything anyone's done to the car is on its page. Leland opens the hood again, and its underside is the record: "Electric fan conversion · Teller Cooling · Jun 2021 · Coldwater Auto · relay under the battery tray." The relay is there, cooked. FMB is shown working from 31.4 s (52 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** a twelve-dollar relay and forty minutes. Leland logs the fix himself, so Two Forks Garage is on the car's page now. "Next stranger who opens this hood won't need me." The fastback rejoins the same road, which is frame 0.
