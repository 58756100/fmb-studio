## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Wendell, eleven hundred miles from home in his '67 Mustang fastback.
2. **Wants:** to get to his brother's wedding on Saturday; the fastback is the getaway car.
3. **But:** it overheats and the only shop in town has never seen it. Leland spots that the electric fan isn't running and that the conversion was wired someone's own way, and he needs to know how.
4. **Therefore:** Wendell opens the fan entry on the car's build page. Coldwater Auto logged the conversion in 2021 with a photo, and the note says where the relay lives: under the battery tray. Leland goes there, tests it, finds it cooked, swaps it, and the fan runs. FMB shown working from 25.4 s (45 %); the find at 35–41 s (65 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Leland logs his own repair, so Two Forks Garage is part of the car's story. "Whoever opens this hood next gets your note too." The fastback rolls back onto the golden road, the frame-0 angle, no steam.
