## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Zeke, a fitter, putting a Corliss fuel pump into a dark green '89 E30.
2. **Wants:** to save the next person the hour he lost.
3. **But:** the tank seal rolled when he twisted the lock ring, so he pencilled "wet the seal first, it rolls" next to step four. He's written that same line on eleven Corliss sheets, and every one went in the bin with the box. Desmond, who wrote the sheet, still thinks step four is fine.
4. **Therefore:** this time Zeke logs the install on the E30's page, and his margin note goes onto the entry word for word, next to the part and the brand. A week later the owner sends Corliss the page to say thanks, and Desmond reads it. FMB is shown working from 21.6 s (43 %). The brand reading it lands at 30.6 s (61 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the next print run of the sheet says it at step four. Zeke opens his next Corliss box, reads step four, and has nothing left to write. His pencil hovers in the margin, which is frame 0.

## 8. Bank check and assets
- **Premise / device:** a carbon copy under an instruction sheet, so the note is copied rather than moved. S0-02 (which airs two hours later on the same evening) lifts Sharpie ink off a wall, so this deliberately doesn't use ink migration, the concept's original device: nothing lifts, floats or migrates. C3-SUP had a support ticket and hang tags, and S0-11 a handwritten slip that unfolds. This has no ticket, no tags, no slip and no thread.
- **Product framing:** the log entry as the second page of a carbon form, then a desk card on Desmond's desk. No phone.
- **Hero pairing:** the E30 with a fuel pump is new (S0-09 used the E30 with an intake, G2 with an engine swap). S0-04 uses the fuel pump on an F-100.
- **Dates:** Oct 2026.
- **Assets:** A10, A11, C19, D06 (the sheet base), F01, F04, E05, E07. The sheet's print, the carbon text and the replay of the seal folding are built in code. **Image requests:** (1) "PHOTO 4:3, gloved hands seating an in-tank fuel pump assembly through the round access hole under the rear seat of a late-1980s BMW coupe, black rubber tank seal and lock ring visible, no text or logos", (2) "CUTOUT, a short yellow wooden pencil, sharpened and slightly worn, three-quarter view, no text or logos", and (3) "PHOTO 9:16, a small office corner inside a parts warehouse, a plain desk under a cork board with blank pinned sheets, tall shelves of unbranded boxes through the doorway, no people, no text".
