## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Gene, in his 60s, with his '67 Mustang fastback and his grandson Cody in the home garage.
2. **Wants:** to put every part of this winter's rebuild on record before a bolt turns.
3. **But:** Cody asks how they'll know what's in there later, and Gene admits half the car only lives in his head.
4. **Therefore:** the knolled bench becomes the build page. Every part gets its brand and today's date. Then number one turns out to be his 1979 engine rebuild, logged with its real date. FMB is shown working from 19.6 s (50 %). The countdown's shocker lands at 27 s (69 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "So the record's older than me?" "Older than your dad." The page folds back into the bench grid, which is frame 0.
