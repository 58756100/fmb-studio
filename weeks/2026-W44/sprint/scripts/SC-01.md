## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Lyle and his guards-red 1993 Porsche 964.
2. **Wants:** respect for the car at tomorrow's show.
3. **But:** for ten years his dash card has said "All original", and it isn't: the engine was rebuilt in 1998 and the wheels came off another car. Tomorrow's judge has owned three 964s and will know.
4. **Therefore:** he could keep the card and hope. Instead he puts the car's real history on its build page, every job with its real date and who did it, and letters new dash cards straight from it, one honest card at a time. FMB is shown working from 22.0 s (55 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the judge skips the paint and asks about the rebuild for twenty minutes. The last card reads "Mostly original. Ask me." The card flips face down on the dash, which is how frame 0 opens (hook C's flip).
