## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Cassie and her 2021 Bronco, at the service desk with a front-end clunk.
2. **Wants:** a fast, cheap diagnosis.
3. **But:** "Anything aftermarket on it?" usually gets "a few things", and last time that answer cost her an hour of labor while the tech found her lift kit the hard way.
4. **Therefore:** this time she hands Marco the truck's page. On the lift, the Bronco lights up like an X-ray, every changed part glowing in the colour of who put it on: Cassie, Trailhead 4x4 or the dealer. Nine aftermarket, fourteen factory, every one dated. FMB is shown working from 20.6 s (45 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Marco finds the clunk in the record: end links, swapped in August, by... Cassie. "Yeah. That one's on me." "Twenty minutes, then. Not an hour." The X-ray fades back to the plain truck on the lift, which is frame 0.
