## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Thad, who owns a small wheel brand, and Kofi, his rep, walking the warehouse aisles.
2. **Wants:** Thad wants to know whether people actually love the wheel, not whether they clicked five stars.
3. **But:** every answer Kofi gives is day-one evidence. Reviews are written the day the box shows up, and "repeat orders" doesn't work, because, as Thad says, nobody reorders a wheel. Each question pushes the ladder, with Kofi on it, one more aisle, until they hit the back wall.
4. **Therefore:** Kofi climbs, and each rung he stands on is a line from a real car's build page: an E30 still on Halvards after two track days, an S2000 that tried another brand and came back, and a WRX whose owner just bought a second set. FMB is shown working from 20.6 s (52 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the WRX disproves Thad's own rule. "...Huh. How many more are out there?" He pushes the ladder back up the aisle toward aisle one, which is frame 0.
