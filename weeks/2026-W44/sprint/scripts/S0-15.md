## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** a torque wrench in a small shop, and Nate's '95 Civic.
2. **Wants:** someone else to know what it knows, namely when and how those red control arms went on.
3. **But:** Nate guesses "last summer, maybe the one before". It was April, the wrench felt every click, and nobody wrote any of it down.
4. **Therefore:** today Tomás re-torques the arms with the car's page open, and every click becomes a line: part, brand, the real April date, a photo. FMB is shown working from 19 s (53 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the last click names the shop, Nate and Tarrow. "Finally. Somebody else knows." The wrench swings back onto the pegboard, which is frame 0.
