## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Wren, driving her silver S2000 to a new shop on a country road.
2. **Wants:** to give the new shop the one date it asked for: her last valve adjustment.
3. **But:** every excuse she's leaned on fails as she drives past it. "I'll remember": she can't pick the year. "The shop has it": her old shop changed systems and lost everything before 2022. "It's in an email somewhere": "valve" returns three hundred newsletters.
4. **Therefore:** the new shop does the adjustment and puts it on the S2000's page, with the date, the clearances and the shop's name. The last sign on the road isn't an excuse; it's the entry. FMB is shown working from 29.0 s (71 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Okay. That's a sign I can actually read." The road loops back to the first sign, "I'll remember", which is frame 0.
