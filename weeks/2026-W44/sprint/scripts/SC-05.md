## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Shay, on a creeper under a customer's silver R34.
2. **Wants:** to tidy up an obvious hack while she's under there: one hand-bent steel bracket where a rubber exhaust hanger should be.
3. **But:** it's welded clean and painted, and hacks are never painted. Somebody meant this.
4. **Therefore:** before she cuts anything she checks the car's page. July 2022: the rubber hanger tore, the muffler dented the diff cover on launch, a solid bracket went on, "don't swap it back". FMB is shown working from 20.2 s (55 %). It's logged by... Shay.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Oh. That was me." "Okay. Leaving it." She rolls back out on the creeper, and the camera rolls back under to the bracket, which is frame 0.
