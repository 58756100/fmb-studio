## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Reggie, a day-shift tech, at 4:58 with a front-mount intercooler half fitted on a blue WRX.
2. **Wants:** to go home and have Inez, on evening shift, finish it without him.
3. **But:** the last handoff cost him three calls at dinner, and the third was the one that mattered: the one thing you always forget to say. Tonight that thing is a stripped bumper bolt he replaced with a longer one.
4. **Therefore:** before the shop rag leaves his hand, the job goes on the car's page: core mounted, piping loose, clamps not torqued, and the bolt, "leave it". Each pass of the rag adds a dated line, printed onto the cloth like a laundry label. FMB is shown working from 17.6 s (41 %); the turn of the story (Inez carries on, and his phone stays quiet) lands at 29.6–33.8 s (69–79 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** next morning Inez hands the rag back. "Piping's done, leak test passed, and it's on there under yours." The rag goes back over the fender, which is frame 0.
