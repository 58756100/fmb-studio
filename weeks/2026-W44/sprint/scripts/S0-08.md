## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Dolores, who runs a shop, with Pete's blue WRX in for a three-day coilover job.
2. **Wants:** to get under the car and finish.
3. **But:** Pete calls every day at four, every call costs twenty minutes, and the answer is always "it's on the lift". The last job went exactly that way.
4. **Therefore:** at drop-off she sends him one link and keeps shooting. Every step lands on the WRX's page as a dated frame: wheel off, coilovers in, alignment. FMB is shown working from 18.6 s (58 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** day two, four o'clock, and the phone doesn't ring. Pete: "Saw the alignment. I'll grab it at five." The strip rewinds to frame 1, drop-off, which is frame 0.
