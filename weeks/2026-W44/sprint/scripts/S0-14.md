## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Omar, who runs a small brand, a month before his suede steering wheel goes on sale.
2. **Wants:** a launch people believe, not a product photo on white.
3. **But:** he has no ad budget and no proof, and he's handing nine wheels to nine builders he can't control. Box seven is going into a Bronco, a trail truck, and he's honestly not sure about that.
4. **Therefore:** he asks for one thing, that each builder logs the install. On launch morning nine build pages already show his wheel, dated and with photos, with Sorrel Works in each parts list. FMB is shown working from 17.4 s (54 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the Bronco he doubted is the one everybody's asking about. The lids close one by one back to nine sealed boxes, which is frame 0.
