## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Joan and her dad Ed, and his '69 Camaro. For forty years Ed has written every job on it in Sharpie on the garage wall.
2. **Wants:** Joan wants to keep the wall, because it's the only copy.
3. **But:** Mom finally won and the garage gets painted Saturday. Ed says he'll remember, but he won't explain a line that just says "'92: don't". Joan's forty photos of the wall turn out to be useless: you can't find the four-speed in a camera roll.
4. **Therefore:** every line goes onto the Camaro's page instead, each with its real date from 1986 on, with Ed reading over her shoulder and correcting her spelling. The ink lifts off the wall into log lines. FMB is shown working from 28.2 s (52 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Saturday the roller covers every date, and it doesn't matter. Ed finally confesses '92 ("I did the timing myself... at night"). "Logged." On the fresh white wall Ed uncaps a Sharpie and writes the first new line, which mirrors frame 0.
