## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Ortiz, who runs a small parts store, answering one question across her counter.
2. **Wants:** to name her best customer, and she's sure she can.
3. **But:** she names Dutch, the one who calls twice a week, and Dutch has bought brake fluid this year. When she's pushed, she admits the register knows what sold and she has no idea where any of it went.
4. **Therefore:** the quiet kid waiting at the counter with a gauge pod says his car is on its page. The label maker, which has printed every answer so far, prints his page entry by entry: six parts over four years, all from her shelf, all on one AE86. FMB is shown working from 28.6 s (57 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Four years of my shelf, on one car." The host asks again, and the label maker prints "ARLO". "Don't tell Dutch." The label maker sits ready for the next question, which is frame 0.
