## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Ortiz, who runs a small parts store, asked one question across her counter.
2. **Wants:** to name her best customer, and she's sure she can.
3. **But:** she names Dutch, who calls twice a week and bought brake fluid this year. Asked whose car she's actually seen her parts on, she admits: nobody's. It all leaves in a box.
4. **Therefore:** the quiet kid at the end of the counter introduces himself and shows her his build page. She reads it and, out of habit, punches each find into her label maker: her clutch, her exhaust, her coilovers, four years on one AE86. FMB shown working from 26.6 s (51 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Four years of my shelf, on one car. And I'd never seen it." The host asks the question again. "Arlo. Don't tell Dutch." The label goes on his gauge box, and the label maker settles in her hand in the frame-0 position.
