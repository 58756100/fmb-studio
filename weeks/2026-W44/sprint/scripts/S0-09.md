## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Tomás, a tech at a small shop, finishing an intake swap on a dark green '89 E30.
2. **Wants:** to button it up before the owner arrives at four.
3. **But:** he's put forty-seven bolts back and there's one more on the fender cover, the oldest fear in any shop. He can't place it, and nobody at his shop has had this car before.
4. **Therefore:** he checks whether the last person on this intake wrote anything down. The owner did: last spring's entry has a photo, and in it the bolt holds the throttle bracket that the new intake now hides. The bolt goes home. FMB is shown working from 17.6 s (55 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Tomás logs today's swap himself, so the next tech gets a short movie. A fresh bolt drops onto a fender cover, which is frame 0.
