## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Ben, who runs returns for a small suspension brand.
2. **Wants:** to know whether this coilover really failed.
3. **But:** the box comes back with a grimy unit and a two-word note, "It failed." No miles, no photos, nothing about the truck. From the part alone he can't tell a bad unit from hard miles, and that usually means a refund and a customer who never buys again.
4. **Therefore:** the slip has one more line, the truck's build page, written by Rick's shop. The record shows it was installed in 2022, has done 30,000 miles since, and had a curb strike in May with no alignment after. FMB is shown working from 21.5 s (51 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "It didn't fail. It got hit." No refund: Ben sends a rebuild at cost and Rick stays. The box closes and the note flips up again, which is frame 0.
