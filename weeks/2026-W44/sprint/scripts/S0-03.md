## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** the host (us, off camera) at a dusk car meet with one question: when did you last do your clutch?
2. **Wants:** a straight answer.
3. **But:** Lou says "spring-ish", then "2019? Maybe 2020?" Tasha says "whenever my buddy was free" and "before we moved". Every answer is a guess, and the guesses run together into one sentence that never ends.
4. **Therefore:** Andre answers with a date and a shop, and the run-on sentence finally gets its full stop. The others go quiet and Lou asks how. It's on his car's page: date, shop, brand, photos. FMB is shown working from 20.4 s (54 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Tasha asks how to start one. Andre turns the mic around: "When'd you do yours?" The host: "...Spring-ish." The sentence starts again at "spring-ish…", which is frame 0.
