## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Mia, on her first day at Colleen's shop.
2. **Wants:** to prove herself on the first car she's given, an R34 that runs hot.
3. **But:** at her last shop, a hot Skyline always meant the radiator. Before lunch she's draining coolant and pricing a nine-hundred-dollar part, which would be her whole first day.
4. **Therefore:** Colleen taps the tool chest without a word. Its drawers open into the car's history: the radiator was replaced six weeks ago by the owner's old shop, with photos. The next drawer shows the hoses are still the factory originals. FMB is shown working from 24.4 s (54 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the split is under the lower clamp, one hose and one hour, and Mia logs the fix under her own name. The drawer slides shut, which is frame 0.
