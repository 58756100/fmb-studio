# P2 — One post, 24 hours

- **Audience:** parts suppliers (performance brands and parts shops), US-first
- **Format:** data reveal (one graph carries the whole story; the reveal is a second line that never flatlines)
- **Runtime target:** 32 s (hook 3 s, body 26 s, CTA 3 s). FMB turn lands at 18 s = 58 %.
- **Learning applied:** "Next tests: named character in first 5 s". Jonah at Vesk Wheels is named at 3 s.
- **Fiction note:** Jonah, Vesk Wheels, the creator, the $2,400 fee, the 38,000 views and the 19 builds are this fictional brand's story, not market data. End frame carries "Brands and people in this story are fictional." Supplier page is shown as a simulation (being built).

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Jonah, who runs Vesk Wheels, a small bronze-wheel brand.
2. **Wants:** people to see his wheels on real cars, so he pays $2,400 for one sponsored post.
3. **But:** the views spike, and 24 hours later the line goes flat for good, while 19 builders who bought Vesks on their own post for free, scattered, and none of it points back to him.
4. **Therefore:** their build pages do. Every parts row that says "Vesk" links to one Vesk page, and every new build adds a car to it: a line that stacks instead of spiking.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Day thirty: the $2,400 post, zero. The page, still counting cars." Loop line: "Before you spend the next $2,400, watch this line." Cut back to the spike.

## 2. Hooks

All three run into body line 1: *"Jonah, Vesk Wheels. One creator, one GR86."*

### Hook A — question
- **Voice (Eric):** "What does $2,400 of attention look like on a graph?"
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** empty white chart axes draw in; a red price tag "$2,400" drops onto the y-axis and swings once.
- **Sentence pair:** "What does $2,400 of attention look like on a graph? Jonah, Vesk Wheels. One creator, one GR86."

### Hook B — specific number
- **Voice (Eric):** "This spike cost $2,400."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** a red line shoots straight up the frame and peaks; rolling-number "$2,400" counts up beside the peak.
- **Sentence pair:** "This spike cost $2,400. Jonah, Vesk Wheels. One creator, one GR86."

### Hook C — contrarian
- **Voice (Eric):** "Sponsored posts don't fail. <break time="0.2s" /> They expire. Watch this one."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** iOS post card with a countdown chip "23:59:59" ticking; the card is already fading at the edges.
- **Sentence pair:** "Sponsored posts don't fail. They expire. Watch this one. Jonah, Vesk Wheels. One creator, one GR86."

## 4. Voice direction
- **Voice:** Eric only. Analyst-calm, almost amused. Stability 0.5, similarity 0.8, style 0.15.
- **Pace:** brisk through the climb (6–9 s), then dead stop at "Flat." Normal through the builders, slight lift on "Until".
- **Emphasis words:** "$2,400", "thirty-eight thousand", "Flat", "free", "Nineteen", "Not one", "Until", "stacks", "zero", "cars".
- **Pauses:** 0.6 s after "Hour twenty-four." (the flatline silence is the reveal); 0.3 s before "Not one pointed back"; 0.3 s before "It stacks." Other gaps 0.22–0.3 s.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **One chart, whole film.** A single animated-line-chart (remocn) is the set: it draws the spike (6–9 s), zooms out to 30 days (9–12 s), and gets a second line at 21 s. Keep the camera on the same chart so the return at 21 s reads as "the same graph, new truth".
2. **9–12 s: the flatline silence.** Freeze all motion for 0.6 s on "Flat." except the pulsing red dot. No music hit; let the room tone carry it.
3. **15–18 s: snapping connections.** Thin grey SVG lines draw from each car tile toward a "Vesk" node and break at 80 % length (2-frame flicker). number-wheel lands "19" in sync with "Nineteen".
4. **18 s: push-through + simulated-cursor.** Push-through into the Miata build page (iOS 26 Safari frame, public URL visible), cursor taps the "Vesk" parts row, tiles fly from the earlier grid to the supplier page with spring easing. Corner tag "Simulation" on the supplier page.
5. **21–24 s: stepping line.** Each new tile landing triggers a one-notch step on the second line plus a soft tick SFX; rolling-number counts 19 → 22 builds. Do not show a per-supplier build map (confirm).
