# C2 — Any service records?

- **Audience:** collectors
- **Format:** POV skit, dialogue only (two voices, no narrator in the body)
- **Runtime target:** 27 s (hook 0–2.5 s, body 2.5–25 s, CTA 25–27 s)
- **Rhythm / device:** staccato texting. Short bubbles, then silence. The typing dots are the villain: every stall is a held breath. The entire film lives in one iMessage thread until the link breaks it open.
- **Learning applied:** dialogue (two voices) vs single narrator test (state/learnings.md). This is the dialogue arm.
- **Characters:** Jay (buyer) and Walt (seller) are fictional. End card carries "Illustrative story".

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Jay, about to book a flight to buy Walt's orange 1972 240Z.
2. **Wants:** to say yes before anyone else does, but only if the car is what Walt says.
3. **But:** he asks for service records, and Walt's answers go soft ("around 2015", "a guy in Reno, he retired"). Then a second 240Z listing pings onto Jay's phone, so Jay starts to walk.
4. **Therefore:** Walt stops typing excuses and sends one link: the build page. The rebuild is there, dated, with photos, logged by the Reno shop itself (FMB shown working, 15–22 s = 56–81 %, landing at 15 s).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Jay: "Deal." Walt: "Saturday?" Then Jay's typing dots appear, the same dots that open the film.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Chris = Jay (buyer):** quick, casual, reads his own texts half under his breath. He clips "Around?" and "By who?" hard. He slows down only when reading the build page (17.5–20.5), like he's actually reading.
- **Brian = Walt (seller):** older, slower, deep. Evasive in 5–11 s (a trailing "Around… 2015?"), then steady and certain from "Hang on". The voice change *is* the turn.
- **Emphasis words:** Around? · retired · Here · May 9th, 2015 · logged · Deal.
- **Gaps:** render with `tts.py --dialogue`. Use 120–180 ms between quick replies; keep the 1.2 s silence at 12–13.2 s untouched by `--tighten`.
- *Italics in the voice column = emphasis direction only; strip before TTS.*

## 7. Motion notes
1. **One-thread world (0–15 s).** Lock the camera on a single imessage-chat-flow. Every reset is a scale or a push, never a cut, so the first real cut (the push-through at 17.5 s) feels huge.
2. **Dots as a character (2.5–15 s).** typing-indicator with a custom pulse. On each stall, remove it on a single frame and kill the audio bed. The 11–15 s stall gets a slow 4 % camera creep.
3. **Banner interrupt (9.5 s).** An iOS 26 glass notification drops from the top with spring overshoot and sits over Walt's last bubble. It's the only time the thread is covered.
4. **Link → page push-through (17.5 s).** The link preview card scales into the full build page (shared-axis-z feel). The orange 240Z cutout in the preview becomes the page header avatar, the same object continuing.
5. **"Deal." stamp (23 s).** The blue bubble with a tiny accent-red tapback (caption-stamp energy, but UI-native). Then match Jay's typing-indicator to frame 0 for the loop.
