# X2 — The record outlives the build

- **Audience:** everyone (collectors, garages, suppliers)
- **Format:** trailer (year title cards, slow deep voice, a silence drop before the turn, the name lands at the end)
- **Runtime target:** 32 s (hook 3 s, body 27 s, CTA 2 s). FMB turn lands at 18 s = 56 %; the name lands at 27 s (brand trailer exception).
- **Learning applied:** "Next tests: named character in first 5 s". The character is the car: "one white Supra" at 3 s. Also tests Brian (trailer voice) against the Eric-narrated pieces.
- **Fiction note:** the Supra's future owners, the shop and Korvane Turbo are fictional; the years 2031–2049 are a story timeline, not predictions. End frame carries "Brands and people in this story are fictional."

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** one white '99 Supra, getting its turbo in 2026.
2. **Wants:** to be understood by whoever owns it next: what was done, by whom, with what.
3. **But:** in 2031 the shop closes, in 2038 the turbo is discontinued, in 2044 the owner sells and the receipts don't make the trip.
4. **Therefore:** the record stays. The build page still shows the shop's name on every job and the brand on every part, at the same public URL.
5. **Payoff + loop:** 2049: a kid opens the link and reads the whole thing. "The record outlives the build." The last title card ("2049") loops back to the hook.

## 2. Hooks

Both run into body line 1 (Brian): *"Twenty twenty-six. One shop knows every bolt."*

### Hook A — contrarian
- **Voice (Brian):** "Your car won't remember who built it."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** pure white frame; Supra car-01 fades up in silence; a single red underline draws under the text "who built it".
- **Sentence pair:** "Your car won't remember who built it. Twenty twenty-six. One shop knows every bolt."

### Hook B — cinematic (flash-forward)
- **Voice (Brian):** "In 2049, someone will buy this Supra <break time="0.3s" /> and ask who built it."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** title card "2049" slams in Inter Black, then the Supra car-03 rear slides in under it, a hand-on-phone silhouette at the edge of frame.
- **Sentence pair:** "In 2049, someone will buy this Supra and ask who built it. Twenty twenty-six. One shop knows every bolt." (Flash-forward, then flashback: classic trailer cadence.)

## 4. Voice direction
- **Voice:** Brian only (deep, trailer). Stability 0.55 for a steadier read, similarity 0.8, style 0.2.
- **Pace:** slow and deliberate, 0.4–0.5 s after each year; no atempo above 1.02 (trailer pacing is the point; cut words before speeding up).
- **Emphasis words:** "won't", "every bolt", "closes", "discontinued", "don't make the trip", "gone", "Except", "still", "whole thing", "outlives".
- **Pauses:** 0.5 s after "Twenty thirty-one" and "Twenty thirty-eight"; 0.6 s before "is gone" (with the music dropping out); 0.5 s after "FuelMyBuild." Other gaps 0.25–0.3 s.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Year title cards as the beat.** Each year ("2026", "2031", "2038", "2044", "2049") lands with the remocn rolling-number from the previous year, in Inter Black at center, on a sub-bass hit. The rolling digits are the trailer's drum.
2. **Loss by subtraction.** Every chapter removes one element from the same frame: the mechanic portrait (particle-dissolve, 6 s), the turbo's color (desaturate, 9 s), the car itself (slide out, 12 s), until the empty white frame at 15 s. Keep the layout fixed so the absence reads.
3. **18 s: push-through from the empty outline** into the build page; the Supra returns at full color. This is the only moment the red accent returns in strength (parts row highlight at 21 s).
4. **24 s: same URL, new hands.** Show the public URL being opened on a phone in 2049; do not show the record being formally transferred to a new owner, since ownership transfer is (confirm).
5. **27 s: kinetic-center-build title,** "FuelMyBuild" then "The record outlives the build." word by word, then hold 0.5 s on white before the CTA and the loop back to the hook frame.
