# C3 — Owner number four

- **Audience:** collectors
- **Format:** mystery / investigation
- **Runtime target:** 30 s (hook 0–2.8 s, body 2.8–27.5 s, CTA 27.5–30 s)
- **Rhythm / device:** three clues counted on screen ("Clue 1/3"), each one stranger than the last, with redacted captions that only unredact when the record answers them. Answers come in reverse, so each solved clue re-opens the next.
- **Learning applied:** named character + car within 5 s.
- **Characters:** Nadia, Hal and Ray are fictional. End card carries "Illustrative story".

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Nadia, owner number four of an orange 1972 240Z. The car is 54 years old, and she knows nothing about the first three owners.
2. **Wants:** to know what she actually bought.
3. **But:** the car doesn't add up. 41,000 miles on a '72, a weld seam painted a shade too new, a strip of tape on the dash: "ask Ray about the motor." There's no Ray in the paperwork, because there is no paperwork.
4. **Therefore:** in the glovebox there's a card with a web address. It opens owner three's (Hal's) build page, where every clue is answered with dates, photos and the shop that did the work (FMB shown working, 17–27.5 s = 57–92 %, landing at 17 s). Hal's last entry hands the record to her (confirm).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "She's yours now. Keep writing." Close: "Owner number five won't have to guess." The final frame is the odometer, which opens the next play.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Clue chips (2.8–25.5 s).** Three persistent pills in the headline band, "Clue 1/3" etc. They redact (caption-redact) at 12.5 s and unredact one by one in the payoff, each with a red check. The viewer tracks progress without being told.
2. **Texture shifts per clue.** Odometer = number-wheel, weld = scribble-circle + shimmer patch, tape = handwrite on paper-wobble. Every clue looks different, which is the visual reset every 3 s.
3. **One dark beat (15–17 s).** The glovebox is the only dark frame in the film. The card slides out into white light, then typewriter + red caret for the URL.
4. **Reverse answers.** Build page entries open in clue order. Each entry uses an iOS 26 sheet with the photo grid first and the date in #86868B under the title.
5. **Loop (27.5 s → 2.8 s).** The small odometer in the end card matches the clue-one zoom target, so the replay feels like re-opening the case.
