# C1 — The shoebox

- **Audience:** collectors
- **Format:** mini story (single narrator + one buyer line)
- **Runtime target:** 29 s (hook 0–2.5 s, body 2.5–26.5 s, CTA 26.5–29 s)
- **Rhythm / device:** slow-burn. A live offer price falls with every faded receipt, then climbs back on the phone. The number is the tension.
- **Learning applied:** named character + car within 5 s (state/learnings.md "Next tests").
- **Characters:** Marcus and the buyer are fictional. End card carries "Illustrative story" in #86868B.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Marcus, selling the white '99 Supra he built over eleven years. The buyer is standing in his garage.
2. **Wants:** his asking price, $68,000, today.
3. **But:** the buyer wants proof of the turbo and coilovers, and the shoebox is full of faded thermal receipts. Every blank one knocks the offer down.
4. **Therefore:** Marcus shuts the box and hands over his phone: the build page, every part with brand and install date, log photos of the work (FMB shown working, 17–23 s = 59–79 %, landing at 17 s).
5. **Payoff + loop:** the buyer scrolls and the offer climbs back to $68,000. "He never opened the shoebox again." The last frame is the lid closing; the first frame is the lid opening.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Offer chip as the spine (5.5–26.5 s).** One persistent rolling-number chip in the headline band. Red only while dropping, grey when dimmed, red again when it locks at $68,000. It's the only accent on screen through the body.
2. **Fading receipts (2.5–11.5 s).** Each receipt is a cream card with real-looking thermal type that particle-dissolves from the edges in as it nears the lens. Use a soft contact shadow, no gradient wash.
3. **Lid-to-phone object swap (14.5–17 s).** The lid's drop drives the phone's rise. Same spring, 4-frame stagger, so the two read as one cause-and-effect gesture.
4. **Build page (17–23.5 s).** iOS 26 inset grouped list for parts, glass sheet for the log entry, floating tab bar. simulated-cursor for the tap, then switch to a thumb-scroll (no cursor) when "the buyer took the phone".
5. **Loop match (26.5 s → 0 s).** Lock the box position and scale between the last body frame and frame 1, so the lid closing → lid opening reads as one continuous take.
