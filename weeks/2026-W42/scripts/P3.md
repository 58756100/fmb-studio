# P3 — Reviews end at install

- **Audience:** parts suppliers (performance brands and parts shops), US-first
- **Format:** myth vs fact (three myths, each stamped and busted with proof; myth three is busted by the turn)
- **Runtime target:** 32 s (hook 3 s, body 26 s, CTA 3 s). FMB turn lands at 17.5 s = 56 %.
- **Learning applied:** "Next tests: named character in first 5 s". Dani and his yellow Civic are named at 3 s.
- **Fiction note:** Dani, his Civic EG and Stellan Suspension are fictional. End frame carries "Brands and people in this story are fictional." Supplier page is shown as a simulation (being built).

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Stellan Suspension, a small coilover brand, and Dani, who bolted a set onto his yellow Civic EG.
2. **Wants:** Stellan wants proof their coilovers hold up, the kind a buyer trusts.
3. **But:** Dani's five-star review was written at zero miles, and when he did come back at 18,000 miles to say they're still perfect, he said it in a group chat that vanished by morning.
4. **Therefore:** the proof lives in Dani's build log: installed March 12, alignment at 18,000, a corner photo at 30,000 on the same coilovers, and the parts row links straight to Stellan's page.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Day one got five stars. Mile thirty thousand got proof." Cut back to the five-star card at zero miles.

## 2. Hooks

All three run into body line 1: *"Dani's Civic hadn't left the driveway. Five stars."*

### Hook A — contrarian
- **Voice (Eric):** "Five-star reviews get written on the one day nobody knows anything."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** five stars pop in one by one in red; on the fifth, a calendar chip slams under them: "Day 1".
- **Sentence pair:** "Five-star reviews get written on the one day nobody knows anything. Dani's Civic hadn't left the driveway. Five stars."

### Hook B — number
- **Voice (Eric):** "Zero miles. <break time="0.2s" /> That's when these coilovers got their five stars."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** coilovers cutout centered; an odometer number-wheel spins and stops on "000000 mi".
- **Sentence pair:** "Zero miles. That's when these coilovers got their five stars. Dani's Civic hadn't left the driveway. Five stars."

### Hook C — myth
- **Voice (Eric):** "Three myths about part reviews. <break time="0.2s" /> The first one's on your product page right now."
- **First 1.5 s on screen:** iOS product page scrolls fast and stops on a review block; a red "Myth 1" stamp hits it.
- **Sentence pair:** "Three myths about part reviews. The first one's on your product page right now. Dani's Civic hadn't left the driveway. Five stars."

## 4. Voice direction
- **Voice:** Eric only. Myth-buster confidence, a little dry on the stamps.
- **Pace:** quick on myth set-ups, a beat slower on each "Busted" and on the turn line.
- **Emphasis words:** "driveway", "box", "come back", "did", "group chat", "never", "nobody", "build log", "same", "proof".
- **Pauses:** 0.3 s after "review of the box"; 0.3 s before "Stellan never saw it"; 0.4 s after "Dani's build log did." (the turn); 0.4 s before "Mile thirty thousand got proof." Other gaps 0.22–0.3 s.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Myth/fact split screen.** A persistent two-panel layout (left "Myth", right "Fact") that flips per myth with a card-flip; "Busted" stamps in the red accent with a 2-frame shake. Myth 3's fact panel stays empty until the turn fills it.
2. **Odometer as the clock.** A number-wheel odometer in the top right is the film's timeline: 0 (3 s) → 18,000 (9 s) → 30,000 (20.5 s). It replaces dates as the thing the eye tracks.
3. **12–15 s: the chat buries the proof.** iOS Messages bubbles stack in fast from the bottom and push Dani's bubble off the top; it particle-dissolves on exit. This is the "show the moment" beat, not "comments get lost".
4. **17.5 s: push-through** from the coilovers cutout into the build page; parts row highlight is the only red element in frame.
5. **23.5 s: simulated-cursor + whip-pan** to the supplier page with a corner "Simulation" tag. Do not show a per-supplier build map (confirm).
