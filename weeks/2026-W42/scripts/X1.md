# X1 — One car, three people

- **Audience:** everyone (collectors, garages, suppliers)
- **Format:** mini story, three POV (three voices, one line each per chapter, whip-pans between them)
- **Runtime target:** 31 s (hook 3 s, body 26 s, CTA 2 s). FMB turn lands at 17.5 s = 56 %.
- **Learning applied:** "Next tests: dialogue (two voices) vs single narrator", pushed to three voices; plus "named character in first 5 s" (Marcus at 3 s).
- **Fiction note:** Marcus, Sal, Kessler Motorsport and Korvane Turbo are fictional (same universe as C1, G1 and P1). End frame carries "Brands and people in this story are fictional."

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** one white '99 Supra and the three people who built it: Marcus (owner), Sal (tech at Kessler Motorsport), Korvane Turbo (the brand).
2. **Wants:** each one wants the car to keep running, and each holds one piece of how it was built.
3. **But:** the wastegate cracks and Marcus needs the exact part, while Sal's specs are on a clipboard that's long gone and Korvane made four versions of that kit.
4. **Therefore:** Marcus sends one link: the build page he's been logging. Sal's install entry is on it under the shop, and the parts row tells Korvane exactly which kit. Right part, shipped next day.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "One car. One record. Three people, finally on the same page." Cut back to the Supra with three name tags, the opening frame.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Voices:** Eric = Korvane (brand, and the hook narrator), Brian = Sal (veteran tech, deep, unhurried), Chris = Marcus (owner, casual). Render with `tts.py --dialogue` so each line is separate with 120–250 ms natural gaps.
- **Pace:** each POV line is one breath; the 15–17.5 s exchange is quick-fire (120 ms gap). The final relay is clipped and even, 150 ms between voices.
- **Emphasis words:** Marcus: "own", "changed", "cracked", "exact", "one link". Sal: "every spec", "drawer", "long gone", "under the shop". Korvane: "shipped", "ends", "four", "which kit", "next day".
- **Pauses:** 0.3 s after "shipped, March 2nd"; 0.3 s after "wastegate cracked"; 0.2 s before "Three people, finally…". Other gaps 0.22–0.3 s.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Chapter cards + whip-pans.** Three chapter tags ("1 · The owner", "2 · The garage", "3 · The brand") in Inter Medium, top left; remocn whip-pan between chapters at 5.8 s and 8.4 s so the voice change and the camera move land on the same frame.
2. **Color-coded POVs without extra colors.** Each POV card has the same white style; only the active speaker's card gets the red outline. The red moves with the voice, which tells viewers who's talking at a glance.
3. **15–17.5 s: three cards meet.** The three POV cards slide into a row with an empty red-outlined slot in each; at 17.5 s the slots fill from the build page (share-sheet → push-through), so the turn reads as the missing pieces arriving.
4. **20.5–26 s: simulated-cursor on live features only.** Garage chip, contributor chip, parts registry row with brand, log entry with date and photo. The brand side is shown as a parts-row tap and an order card; no per-supplier build map (confirm).
5. **26–29 s: kinetic-center-build relay.** "One car. / One record. / Three people, finally on the same page." builds word by word at center, one voice per line, then dissolves into the opening Supra frame for the loop.
