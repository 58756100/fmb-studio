# P1 — After checkout

- **Audience:** parts suppliers (performance brands and parts shops), US-first
- **Format:** challenge / investigation (clue counter, dead ends, reveal)
- **Runtime target:** 32 s (hook 2.5 s, body 26.5 s, CTA 3 s). FMB turn lands at 17.5 s = 56 %.
- **Learning applied:** "Next tests: named character in first 5 s". The turbo has an order number and a brand (Korvane) inside 3 s; the Supra and Kessler arrive at the reveal.
- **Fiction note:** Korvane Turbo, order A-2207, the Ohio Supra owner and Kessler Motorsport are fictional. End frame carries "Brands and people in this story are fictional." Supplier page is shown as a simulation (the feature is being built).

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Korvane Turbo, a small turbo brand, and one kit: order A-2207.
2. **Wants:** to know where that kit went and whether it's still making boost.
3. **But:** tracking ends at "Delivered", the tagged posts don't say whose turbo, and the only review says "Arrived fast."
4. **Therefore:** we stop searching for the buyer and search for the part. The build log finds it: a white '99 Supra, parts row "Turbo kit · Korvane", installed March 14 at Kessler Motorsport, still logging, linked back to Korvane's page.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Their receipt says delivered. Now they know where it went." The last frame cuts back to the "Delivered" dot the video opened on.
