# G1 — The notes left with him

- **Audience:** garages
- **Format:** mini story
- **Runtime target:** 28 s (hook 0–2.5 s, body 2.5–26 s, CTA 26–28 s)
- **Rhythm / device:** clock-driven. A rolling timestamp in the headline band (Fri 5:42 pm → Mon 7:58 am → 8:02 → noon) that jumps forward at every beat. Each jump is a re-hook, and the stakes are a deadline.
- **Learning applied:** named character + shop + car within 5 s.
- **Characters:** Dave, Leo and Kessler Motorsport are fictional illustrations (the shop name comes from the brief). End card carries "Illustrative story".

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Leo, a second-year tech at Kessler Motorsport, the Monday after lead tech Dave quit.
2. **Wants:** the white '99 Supra fixed and back to its owner by Wednesday.
3. **But:** it's back on lift two with a boost leak on the turbo Dave installed. Dave knew how it was done, Dave isn't answering, and the owner is already texting.
4. **Therefore:** Leo opens the car's build log. Dave logged every job, including a photo labeled "rerouted vacuum line — check here first," signed with his name (FMB shown working, 15.5–23.5 s = 55–84 %, landing at 15.5 s).
5. **Payoff + loop:** fixed in four minutes, out by noon. "Dave's gone. His notes stayed." The final frame cuts to Dave's keys on the counter, Friday 5:42 pm, which is the hook's first frame.

3. **Call screen (8.5–11.5 s).** An authentic iOS 26 incoming/outgoing call layout. End the call on a hard 1-frame cut to near-silence plus a single "call ended" tone.
