## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Tess runs marketing at Calder Wheels and is launching a bronze wheel.
2. **Wants:** real cars on her wheels for the launch, proof a buyer believes.
3. **But:** she believes three myths: builders don't document, they never name the brand, and even if they did, you'd never find it. So she books a staged shoot for six thousand dollars. Meanwhile Jules is fitting her bronze wheels to his blue WRX, photographing every bolt and typing "Calder" into the brand field.
4. **Therefore:** Jules tags Calder with one link. It's his build page: "Wheels · Calder" in the parts registry, a dated install, photos, on a public URL (FMB shown working). All three myths go down on her own screen.
5. **Payoff + loop:** Tess cancels the shoot and shares his page instead. "Builders document." answers the hook "Tess thinks builders don't document" and restarts the film.
