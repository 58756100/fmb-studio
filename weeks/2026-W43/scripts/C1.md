# C1 — The appraiser's question

- **Audience:** collectors
- **Format:** mini story
- **Runtime target:** 29.6 s (hook 0–2.8 s, body 2.9–25.1 s, CTA 25.3–28.3 s, end card 28.3–29.6 s). Hard cap 30 s.
- **Rhythm / device:** a value chip ("Agreed value $86,000") sits in the corner of the video call the whole film. It drops when the proof fails and rolls back when the record lands, so the stakes stay on screen.
- **Learning applied (2026-10-18 pre-flight diagnostics):** named character + car inside 5 s; hook <=2.5 s, no internal pause; body line 1 at hook pace (3.1 w/s) with no pause over 0.3 s in the first 4 s of body; the only deliberate silences come after 12 s; runtime capped under 30 s with the pipeline overheads counted.
- **Characters:** Elena, the appraiser and Mesa Import are fictional. A small "Illustrative story" label sits bottom-left from 0 s, and the end card repeats it.
- **Compliance:** we don't claim any insurer accepts FMB pages as proof. One fictional appraiser accepts dated photos, which is the story.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Elena has owned her black 1991 NSX for eight years.
2. **Wants:** her agreed-value renewal to count the clutch job she paid for in March 2024.
3. **But:** on the video call the appraiser wants proof. The shop has closed, the receipt is gone, and his cursor drifts toward "not verified", which knocks $8,000 off the value.
4. **Therefore:** she remembers she logged the job and pastes her build page link into the call chat. The dated log entry, with photos of the old and new disc and the clutch kit (with brand) in the parts registry, does the arguing for her. FMB is on screen at 16.4 s (55 %) and the page unfurls at 16.9 s (57 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** he clicks "verified" and the chip rolls back to $86,000: "Eight grand, kept by one photo." The final frame returns to the call tile, where the appraiser's muted lips form "prove it" again, so the replay drops straight back into the hook.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Eric** narrates (measured 2.6 w/s at speed 1.0). **Chris** voices the appraiser in hook C only. Justin is Wayne's pick for collector stories, but he isn't in `extract_vo.py` ROSTER yet, so Eric is cast. Swap only after Justin is added and measured.
- **Seam pace:** render hook A/B and body row 1 at `--speed 1.2` (2.6 × 1.2 ≈ 3.1 w/s). Render rows 2–8 at 1.0 and let assembly atempo them to about 2.7 w/s (max 1.08×). The ear must not hear a slowdown at 2.9 s.
- **Pauses:** rows 1–3 render with `--tighten` (commas capped at 0.16 s, full stops at 0.26 s). Row 4 (the 0.6 s silence after "Then,") and row 7 (0.3 s before the click) render **without** `--tighten`, or it will clip the breaks. Every break sits inside a line, because `assemble_reel.py` trims a segment's leading and trailing silence. There are no break tags before 12 s.
- **Seam silence:** the current pipeline puts 0.32 s of hook tail + 0.10 s of body lead = 0.42 s at the seam, which breaks the 0.3 s rule. Set the hook tail to 0.15 s for W43 (seam 0.25 s). The budget above still assumes 0.32, so the cut only frees 0.17 s.
- **Emphasis words:** prove (row 1) · closed · not verified · remembers · chat · verified · kept.
- **Tone:** brisk, quietly tense for the first half, then audibly relieved from "One link" on. Not salesy.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Value chip (2.9–25.1 s).** A persistent pill in the top-right of the call frame. It ghosts to $78,000 at 3.5 s and 11 s, and rolls back with a number-wheel at 23 s. This is the stakes meter.
2. **Three textures before the turn.** Call UI (2.9 s), map pin + paper receipts (7 s), form + cursor (10 s): each beat looks different.
3. **The silence (13.0–13.6 s).** Hold on the cursor with no music swell, then cut to Elena's eyes. This is the only dead-still frame in the film.
4. **The turn is a chat bubble.** The URL types in the call chat and unfurls into the build page. FMB never appears as a logo before 16.4 s.
5. **Proof shot.** In the log entry, photos come first and the date in #86868B sits under the title. The registry row has the brand slot in brand red.
