- **Features shown (all live):** build page, log entry with date and photo, public URL. Luis's shop keeps the Mustang's build page; the link is sent by ordinary text message. No approval, notification or invoicing feature is shown or implied. No (confirm) items.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Luis, who runs a two-bay shop. Gary's blue Mustang GT is in for a brake job.
2. **Wants:** to get paid for the work he did and keep Gary as a customer.
3. **But:** mid-job he found rotted brake lines and added $480. Gary says "you never told me." Luis did call him on Tuesday, but a phone call leaves no proof, and Gary is threatening a one-star review.
4. **Therefore:** rather than give in, Luis opens the Mustang's build page. Tuesday's dated log entry shows a photo of Gary's own brake line, rusted through, with Luis's note "Called Gary, OK to replace." He texts Gary the public link (FMB shown working, 15.8–21.8 s = 56–77 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** Gary pays, then asks "What else is rusty?" The invoice chip reads "$480 · Paid", which matches the "$480" that opens the film, so the replay starts on the same number.
