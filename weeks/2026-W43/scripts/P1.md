# P1 — What turbo is that?

- **Audience:** parts suppliers (performance brands and parts shops), US-first
- **Format:** countdown with escalating stakes (three wrong guesses, 3 → 1; number one is the shocker). New format (explore).
- **Runtime target:** 29 s (hook 0–1.8 s, body 1.8–25.8 s, CTA 25.8–29.1 s). Hard cap 30 s. FMB turn lands at 16.9 s; the build page is on screen at 17.6 s = 60 %.
- **VO budget:** 72 spoken words (hook A 5 + body 58 + CTA 9). Estimated runtime 29.1 s (see section 3a).
- **Rhythm / device:** a countdown chip ("Guess 3", "2", "1") over a comment thread that grows more confident and more liked with every wrong answer. The likes counter is the stakes meter.
- **Learning applied (2026-10-18 pre-flight diagnostics):** hook A 1.7 s; body line 1 at 3 w/s with one 0.15 s break; no pause over 0.2 s before 12 s; first deliberate silence at 13.9 s; runtime under 30 s. Named character (Kenji) in the hook, car (R34) at ~3 s.
- **Characters:** Kenji, Akane Turbo, the R34 owner and the brands Vantor, Grindell and Ostra are fictional. Corner tag "Illustrative story" throughout; end card repeats it.
- **Live features shown:** build page with public URL, parts registry with brand per part, log entries with dates and photos. No (confirm) features.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Kenji, who makes turbos in a small shop under his own brand, Akane Turbo.
2. **Wants:** credit. A silver R34 post blows up, and the top comment is "what turbo is that?? 👀". That turbo is his.
3. **But:** the comments answer for him, and they get it wrong in ascending confidence: Vantor (12 likes), Grindell (89 likes), and then the shocker: Ostra, the rival brand's own account, replies "Looks like ours." 412 likes. His turbo now belongs to someone else.
4. **Therefore:** Kenji doesn't argue. The post caption carries the owner's public build page. He opens it, and the parts registry already says "Turbo · Akane Turbo", with a dated install entry and photos (FMB shown working). He replies with the link. Ostra goes quiet.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Let the build answer." The last frame is the original "what turbo is that??" comment, now with one reply: the link. Replay opens on the same comment.

## 7. Motion notes
1. **Countdown chip (4.6–13.9 s).** Top-left pill, number-wheel flip 3 → 2 → 1; "1" turns red. Each wrong guess gets a red "Wrong" stamp (scale 1.15 → 1.0, 2-frame shake) synced to the word timing, not the beat estimate.
2. **Likes as the stakes meter.** One like counter per bubble, each bigger than the last: 12, 89, 412. The 412 spin fills the only early silence (13.9–14.4 s).
3. **One thread, three textures.** Commenter bubbles are standard iOS grey; the rival's bubble has a brand avatar and a faint shimmer; Kenji's reply carries the URL card with a red edge. The eye always knows who is talking.
4. **Turn (16.9 s).** Simulated cursor on the caption link → push-through into the iOS Safari frame with the public URL. The registry row highlight is the only red element on the build page.
5. **Loop (25.8 s → 0 s).** End on the original comment with the link reply under it, framed exactly like the hook's first frame.
