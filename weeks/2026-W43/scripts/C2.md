- **Audience:** collectors
- **Format:** countdown with escalating stakes (new this week)
- **Runtime target:** 29.0 s (hook 0–2.8 s, body 2.9–24.7 s, CTA 24.9–27.9 s, end card 27.9–29.0 s). Hard cap 30 s.
- **Rhythm / device:** a giant numeral (3, 2, 1) slams in at each question, and a "Deposit pending · $9,000" chip sits under the text thread. Each question is harder than the last, and the last one stalls him.
- **Learning applied (2026-10-18 pre-flight diagnostics):** body pace kept at hook pace through the seam (row 1 at 3.1 w/s, rows 2+ at 2.7 w/s); no pause over 0.3 s in the first 4 s of body; hook <=2.5 s; the only deliberate silence is at 13.4 s; named character + car inside 5 s.
- **Characters:** Hank, the buyer and Dorsey's Garage are fictional. A small "Illustrative story" label sits bottom-left from 0 s, and the end card repeats it.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Hank is selling his green 1988 E30 325is to a buyer he's never met. The $9,000 deposit is pending.
2. **Wants:** the deposit, today.
3. **But:** the buyer's questions get sharper. Paint is easy, and the timing belt he answers from memory ("Hank swears"). That hedge makes the buyer go for the throat: "Who did the head gasket?" Hank freezes and guesses: a buddy? 2019?
4. **Therefore:** he stops guessing and sends his public build URL. The log answers better than his memory did: the gasket was done by Dorsey's Garage in June 2021, with photos (FMB on screen at 17.6 s, 61 %).
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Deposit sent." "No fourth question." The end card resets the numeral to "3", so the replay restarts the countdown.
