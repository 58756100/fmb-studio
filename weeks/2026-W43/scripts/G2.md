# G2 — Fourteen entries

- **Audience:** garages
- **Format:** data reveal (one counter that changes how you see a "small" shop)
- **Runtime target:** 28.8 s (hook 0–2.0 s, body 2.0–25.1 s, CTA 25.1–28.8 s). Hard cap 30 s.
- **Rhythm / device:** two counters. First, a stack of paper oil-change windshield stickers that grows to 14 (2.0–4.8 s, analog). At the turn, the stack flips into the build page's live counter row: "14 entries · 31 photos · 1 public URL" (16.2 s, digital). The same number appears twice in two textures, and the second one is the reveal.
- **Learnings applied (2026-10-18 pre-flight diagnostics):** body line 1 at hook pace (3.2 w/s, brief's "explore" item for G2); hook 2.0 s; no pause over 0.1 s before 12 s; first deliberate silence at 12.6 s; VO counted per beat. Named character (Tasha) and car (Kofi's yellow Civic) at 2–4.8 s.
- **Characters:** Tasha (owner of a one-bay quick-lube), Kofi (Civic owner), Priya (stranger with an EK hatch) and the yellow '93 EG Civic are fictional. End card carries "Illustrative story".
- **Features shown (all live):** build page, log entries with dates, mileage written in the entry text, photos, public URL. Kofi shares the link in a Facebook-style group (an ordinary social post). Priya books **by phone**. No booking, lead or messaging feature is shown or implied. No (confirm) items.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Tasha, who runs a one-bay oil-change shop. For two years she has logged every oil change on Kofi's yellow '93 Civic on its build page, with date, mileage and photos.
2. **Wants:** real work (clutches, timing belts), not only $49 oil changes.
3. **But:** nobody books a lube shop for real work. Customers ask "Just oil, right?" So she keeps logging, and nobody reads it.
4. **Therefore:** Kofi drops the Civic's public link in a Civic owners' group. A stranger, Priya, opens it: 14 entries, 31 photos, every mile accounted for (FMB shown working, 16.2–22.0 s = 56–76 %). She reads all fourteen and calls.
5. **Payoff + loop:** Tasha's first clutch job, and a new build page at "Entry 1". The counter reads "1", which loops back to "Fourteen entries" at frame one.
