## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Ana, support lead at Velden Suspension, a small coilover brand.
2. **Wants:** to treat customers fairly, which means knowing what actually happened to the part.
3. **But:** Velden ships and then hears nothing, ever. The first time a set comes back into view is a warranty claim: a leaking shock on Owen's Evo VI with 41,000 miles and no history. Installed when? Were those miles on Velden's set? Deny it and she loses Owen; approve it and she's guessing.
4. **Therefore:** Owen sends his build page. The parts registry says "Coilovers · Velden", installed at 12,000 miles, and every service since is a dated log entry with photos (FMB shown working). 29,000 of the miles were on Velden's set.
5. **Payoff + loop:** approved the same day. "First install we ever saw." The final frame returns to the claim card's "41,000 mi", now with every field filled, which reopens the hook.
