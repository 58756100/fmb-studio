## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Jun owned a red 1993 RX-7 for six years and sold it last month.
2. **Wants:** to give the new owner, Sam, a straight answer: who rebuilt the engine? On a rotary, that answer is most of the car's value.
3. **But:** Jun paid cash, kept nothing and forgot the shop's name. He texts back "I don't remember", and it shows as seen.
4. **Therefore:** he rebuilds the record from his camera roll, entry by entry, on a build page for the RX-7 (FMB on screen at 16.4 s, 57 %). Putting the photos in date order surfaces the October 2019 shot with a shop sticker on the intake: Halvorsen Rotary. He sends Sam the page.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Next car? Logged from day one." His new build page opens with entry 1. The final frame is a fresh text bubble with "Hey Jun, …" typing, the same frame that opens the film.

- **Seam pace:** hooks and row 1 at the auditioned speed for at least 3.0 w/s. Rows 2+ at 1.0 and let assembly atempo them to 2.7 w/s.
