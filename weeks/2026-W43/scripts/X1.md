# X1 — Dad's AE86

- **Audience:** everyone
- **Format:** letter read-aloud (new this week)
- **Runtime target:** 29.6 s (hook 0–2.8 s, body 2.9–25.1 s, CTA 25.4–28.3 s, end card 28.3–29.6 s). Hard cap 30 s.
- **Rhythm / device:** Nora reads her father's letter aloud, and halfway through, his voice takes the letter over from her. The handwritten page is the stage. Every line of the letter opens a question the next line deepens, and the last line hands her (and us) a URL.
- **Learning applied (2026-10-18 pre-flight diagnostics):** named character + car inside 5 s ("Nora," at 2.9 s, "AE86" by 4.9 s, and the lower third from 0.3 s); hook <=2.5 s; body line 1 at 3.0 w/s, one sentence with commas only through 6.9 s; first deliberate silence at 12.4 s; runtime under the 30 s cap.
- **Characters:** Nora and her father Ken are fictional. A small "Illustrative story" label sits bottom-left from 0 s, and the end card repeats it.
- **Tone guard:** grief is the backdrop, not the product. No sad-piano cliché, no "in loving memory" card, no tears on screen. The warmth comes from the reveal, not from sorrow.

## 1. Story spine
1. **Who:** Nora (27) has just inherited her late father's panda (white-over-black) 1986 Corolla GT-S AE86.
2. **Wants:** to know the car the way he did. His letter, folded in the glovebox, promises to tell her.
3. **But:** the letter raises the question every AE86 person asks about this car, "who did the diff?", and admits he never told anyone.
4. **Therefore:** his voice takes over: he wrote it down instead, and it's all on the page. The URL at the foot of the letter opens his build page (FMB on screen at 16.2 s, 55 %; page open at 17.0 s, 57 %). The log entry for the diff, May 2008, has one photo: Dad, and a nine-year-old holding the wrench.
5. **Payoff + loop:** "Me. It was me." She was the answer all along. The final frame is Nora sliding a folded note of her own into the glovebox and closing it, which matches the opening glovebox frame for the replay.

## 4. Voice direction
- **Nora:** a clear, warm, younger female voice. In the files she is labelled `Rita (Nora)`, because `extract_vo.py` ROSTER only knows Eric, Brian, Chris and Rita (Rita = ElevenLabs premade Sarah). Wayne's pick for female characters is Emma, who isn't in the extractor yet. If Emma is added before render, relabel. Otherwise Sarah reads Nora.
- **Brian as Dad (Ken):** deep, unhurried, a little gruff, with a smile under it. He takes over the letter mid-read (rows 3–4) and never returns. That's the point.
- **Seam pace:** Nora reads row 1 briskly, like she's skimming for the important part, not reciting: at least 3.0 w/s, the same speed as the hook. Audition Sarah at speed 1.1–1.2 for hook + row 1. The letter only slows down once Dad's voice arrives (after 10.6 s).
- **Pauses:** rows 1–2 with `--tighten`. Rows 3, 6 and 7 carry deliberate breaks (12.4 s, 19.8 s, 23.6 s) and render **without** `--tighten`. Every break sits inside a line, because assembly trims a segment's leading and trailing silence.
- **Seam silence:** the current 0.32 s hook tail + 0.10 s body lead = 0.42 s, so set the hook tail to 0.15 s for W43. The budget assumes 0.32.
- **Emphasis words:** yours · diff · instead · page · photo · wrench · me.
- **"Me. It was me.":** not crying. A small, astonished laugh on the second "me".
