I run FuelMyBuild (fuelmybuild.com), a platform where car builders document
their builds: build pages, a parts registry with brands, a public URL, log
entries and photos. Audiences: collectors, garages, parts suppliers. US-first.
We speak as "we".

We make short vertical videos (IG Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) with an AI
studio built on Claude. Each week it ideates, writes scripts with 2-3 hook
variants, generates ElevenLabs voiceovers, animates and renders the visuals,
runs QA and schedules posts with automated routines. Every Sunday it pulls
the metrics (retention curve, watch time, views, likes, comments, shares,
saves) and rewrites its playbook. Current problem: hooks hold 3-7 seconds,
then viewers drop. Target: average watch time of 10+ seconds and real comments.

You're joining a controlled test against Claude. You handle the creative
side. Claude makes ALL visuals and renders, so describe visual intent and
don't produce images or video. There are 3 lanes:
A) BLIND: create from scratch, from this brief and what's working on
   short-form right now.
B) INFORMED: I give you our learnings, scripts, VO notes and visual refs,
   and you make your own better versions.
C) CRITIC: you review Claude's finished videos and their metrics and say
   exactly what to change.
Every lane gets the same topics, slots and pipeline, and the results are
judged on real metrics.

Don't produce anything yet. Reply with:
1. 5 lines on what you understand the goal to be.
2. Up to 5 questions you need answered before lane A.
3. Confirmation that you'll deliver every video in this exact format:

   ID / AUDIENCE / TOPIC

   VO DIRECTION: voice type, pace (wpm), energy, pauses
   CAPTION + 3-5 hashtags + CTA
   RETENTION LOGIC: why viewers stay past 3s, 5s, 10s, and the comment trigger
