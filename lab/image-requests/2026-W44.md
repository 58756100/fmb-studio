# Image requests, W44 lab (lanes B and C)

Paste the MASTER PROMPT once into a new ChatGPT chat, then send each image prompt one message at a time. Download each file, rename it to the given filename, zip them all and give the zip to Claude.

Claude will check each one (real transparency, size, no text or logos) and put them in `kit/remotion/public/img/obj/`. Anything that fails gets a corrected prompt.

We already have: car cut-outs (incl. orange 240Z, red NA Miata, white '99 Supra with bronze wheels, green E30), turbo kit, brake kit, coilovers, wheel, intake, exhaust, mechanic portrait, shoebox, receipts, shipping boxes, engine block, car lift, phone in hand, clipboard, price tag, review stars. So only the images below are new.

## MASTER PROMPT (paste once)

```
You're generating production images for short vertical car videos. I'll send
one image request per message. Rules for EVERY image:

1. ONE image per request. Never a grid, collage, sheet, set or variations.
   One subject, one file.
2. Two types:
   - CUTOUT: a PNG with a REAL transparent background (alpha channel). Not
     white, not grey, and not a checkerboard pattern drawn into the image.
     Whole subject in frame with about 5% empty margin on every side. No floor,
     no cast shadow, no reflection, no props unless I list them.
   - PHOTO: a full-frame image, no transparency, in the aspect ratio I give.
     It should look like a sharp phone photo taken in a clean, well-lit
     independent garage.
3. Photorealistic. Soft studio key light from the upper left, gentle fill,
   true-to-life colours, crisp detail. Never illustration, 3D-render gloss,
   cartoon or painterly.
4. No text, numbers, logos, brand names, badges, stickers, licence plates or
   watermarks anywhere. Parts must be unbranded.
5. No faces unless I ask for a person. Hands in work gloves are fine.
6. Largest size you can make: at least 1536 px on the long side.
7. Car models and colours must match what I describe exactly; the same car
   appears across several images.

Reply "ready" and wait for the first request.
```

- Photos: JPG at 4:3 (or 4:5 where stated), at least 1536 px on the long side.
- No readable text, logos or plates anywhere; car models and colours match.
- Filenames exactly as listed.
