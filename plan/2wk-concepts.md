# Two-week sprint concept map (Oct 26 – Nov 8, 2026)

Sources: plan/2wk-sprint-2026-10-26.md, state/production-standards.md, state/visual-bank.md, state/learnings.md, ideation skill, audience tensions, lab/image-requests/image-bank-v2.md.

Totals: 25 Claude concepts (15 lane 0 `S0-`, 10 lane C `SC-`), 20 reserved ChatGPT slots (10 lane A `LA-`, 10 lane B `LB-`), plus the 9 lab reels as bodies. That is 54 bodies. The day-by-day schedule is in `plan/2wk-calendar.md`.

Claude concepts by audience: collectors 8, garages 8, suppliers 7, everyone 2. All US-first.

Rules that apply to every concept:
- A named character in the first 5 s. This tests the open learning "named character vs problem statement". The two-voice skits (S0-05, S0-10, SC-07) also test "dialogue vs single narrator".
- Product features are live only: build pages, parts registry with brand, public URL, log entries (past dates allowed), photos, garage and contributor attribution. Nothing needs (confirm).
- No ownership-transfer flow, no supplier-page promise. Supplier CTAs use "DM PARTS and we'll show you where your parts end up".
- Numbers inside a story belong to the fictional character (his shop, her list). No market statistics anywhere.
- Hero cars avoid the Supra and 240Z. Image-bank ids: A = hero cars (3 shots each), B = showroom, C = parts, D = props, E = people, F = places, G = work-in-progress photos.
- Story device is new to the bank. No phone-frame push-through as spine, no rolling-number spine, no bubble thread, no identical-A/B pair, no red dashed line, no shoebox, no wall clock.
- Length is 30 to 60 s, set by the story.

## Lane 0 (Claude, 15)

### S0-01 Knolling Day
- **Audience:** collectors
- **Premise:** Before a winter rebuild, a collector lays every part out in a neat grid and decides the record starts before the first bolt turns.
- **Character / moment / turn:** Gene (E01), 60s, '67 fastback. His grandson (E03) holds up a socket and asks "how will we know what's in there?" The turn: the laid-out parts become the build page. Each part is logged with brand and a date, and the plan becomes the record.
- **Format:** countdown (five parts, five to one)
- **Story device:** knolling, where parts sit at right angles and each one slides off the bench onto the car as it is logged.
- **Length:** 35 s. One idea with a countdown doing the work.
- **Hooks:** (1) specific number: "Five parts. One winter. Gene wants every one on record." (2) in-medias-res: "Gene hasn't touched a bolt yet, and he's already keeping the record." (3) question: "What if the build log started before the build?"
- **CTA family:** share/save or link
- **Assets:** A16 (+A17), C05, C14, D04, F02, E01, E03
- **Nearest existing and why this differs:** C1 / C1-v2 (shoebox, a sale, paperwork already lost). This is before the work, a family scene, no sale, no lost paper, and the camera lives on a flat-lay grid.

### S0-02 The Wall of Sharpie
- **Audience:** collectors
- **Premise:** A man's decades of car dates are written on his garage wall, and the wall is about to be painted.
- **Character / moment / turn:** Ed (E01) and his daughter Joan (E02). Joan sees the paint roller waiting. The turn: she photographs each scrawl and enters it with its real past date. The roller then covers the wall and the record stays.
- **Format:** mini story
- **Story device:** ink migration. The handwriting lifts off the wall and settles into log lines.
- **Length:** 50 s. It needs a setup, a threat, a turn and a payoff.
- **Hooks:** (1) curiosity gap: "This wall has forty years of Ed's Camaro written on it, and tomorrow it's getting painted." (2) visual shock: the roller strokes across a date in silence, no voice for 1 s. (3) specific number: "The first date on this wall is 1986."
- **CTA family:** link or done-for-you
- **Assets:** A19, A20, C14, F02, G11, E01, E02
- **Nearest existing and why this differs:** X2 (loss by subtraction, trailer) and C1 (paper). No sale, no loss at the end, and a physical wall where the record is saved by moving the ink.

### S0-03 Curb Questions
- **Audience:** collectors
- **Premise:** A host asks owners at an evening meet "when did you last do your clutch?" and gets five honest guesses and one date.
- **Character / moment / turn:** Four owners answer by memory ("spring-ish", "my buddy did it"). The fifth, Dev (E03), answers with an exact date and the shop's name. The turn: the guessers go quiet and one asks how.
- **Format:** interview-style (street)
- **Story device:** the answers are cut into one long run-on sentence of guesses that ends on a date.
- **Length:** 40 s. Four quick takes plus a payoff.
- **Hooks:** (1) question you can't answer: "When did you last change your clutch? Be honest." (2) entertainment: "Five owners, one clutch, zero dates." (3) direct callout: "If you've ever said 'it was around 2019'..."
- **CTA family:** share/save
- **Assets:** A37, C13, F06, E01, E02, E03
- **Nearest existing and why this differs:** A-L3 / B-L3 (a guess hardens into fact in a thread). Here nobody's guess hardens. The guesses stay guesses, there is no comment thread, and the format is a spoken street interview.

### S0-04 Backdate in 60
