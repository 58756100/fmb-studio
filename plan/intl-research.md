# International markets: where FuelMyBuild goes after the US

Research date: 2026-10-17. Scope: which 3–5 non-US markets fit a car-build documentation platform (build page, public link, parts registry with brands, dated log entries with photos, garage/contributor credit, done-for-you setup), and how to speak to each in its own language.

Numbers are the best public figures found. Where a figure comes from a vendor, broker or market-research seller, it is marked as such. Social figures are DataReportal ad-reach estimates for January 2025 (not unique users).

## 2. United Kingdom (rank 1)

### Why it fits
- Historic-vehicle sector worth **£7.3bn a year** and 34,500 jobs ([FBHVC National Historic Vehicle Survey 2025](https://www.fbhvc.co.uk/news/article/britains-love-of-historic-vehicles-powers-73-billion-boost-to-the-uk-economy), [survey page](https://fbhvc.co.uk/survey)). DVLA historic vehicles reportedly at ~1.93m (quoted from the report on a [forum](https://www.theminiforum.co.uk/forums/topic/364676-the-national-historic-vehicle-survey-2025); check the PDF before quoting on screen). Average owner is 66, but under-35 interest is growing, especially in "youngtimers".
- **Auction and provenance culture:** Iconic Auctioneers alone sold £35m of collector cars in 2025 (976 cars, 83% sell-through) ([Motor Trade News](https://www.motortradenews.com/?p=72143)). Hagerty UK says the boom is over and buyers are cautious, with ~80% of tracked values flat or down ([Hagerty UK](https://www.hagerty.co.uk/articles/analysis-classic-car-market-2025/)). Commentators say cars with good maintenance histories still sell well. In a soft market, the documented car is the one that sells.
- **Independent garages:** 30,000+ independents in a £31.7bn service and repair market ([Whito](https://whito.co.uk/garages/research/uk-automotive-marketing-data/), marketing estimate). IGA membership grew 22% in 2025 to 4,500+ ([PMM](https://pmmonline.co.uk/?p=43945)).
- **Insurance declaration:** in a specialist broker's survey, 52% of drivers didn't know they must declare all modifications ([Adrian Flux via Insurance Edge](https://insurance-edge.net/2022/06/12/drivers-unaware-they-must-declare-vehicle-mods/); the broker sells modified-car cover). An undeclared mod can void a policy.
- **MOT:** cars over 40 years old can skip the MOT only if there has been no "substantial change" to the main components ([gov.uk criteria](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/historic-classic-vehicles-mot-exemption-criteria/historic-classic-vehicles-mot-exemption-criteria)). Owners of modified classics need to know, and show, what was changed and when.
- **Social:** Instagram reaches 33.4m users (59.5% of adults); TikTok reaches 24.8m adults (45.0%) ([DataReportal UK 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-united-kingdom)). These are the highest adult reach figures of any candidate.

### Language and register
- British English. Re-voice with a British voice (ElevenLabs), not a US voice reading UK words.
- Register: dry, understated, a little self-deprecating. Hype reads as American and gets mocked. "Honestly, it's the folder that sells the car" beats "This will change your life."
- Words to use: *motor, project, mods, declared mods, history file, full service history (FSH), stamped book, receipts, V5C (logbook), MOT, garage / independent, spannering / fettling, restomod, petrolhead, track day, bhp, miles, tyres, bonnet, boot, wing (not fender), exhaust (not muffler), petrol (not gas).*
- Avoid US terms: gas, hood, trunk, fender, muffler, tire, "shop" for a garage (say "garage" or "workshop"), dollar pricing.

### Top pain points
| Audience | Pain points |
|---|---|
| Collectors | 1) The history file is a lever-arch folder that can be lost, damaged or left with a seller. 2) In a softer auction market, buyers discount cars with gaps in their history. 3) Listing every mod accurately for the insurer or an agreed-value valuation. |
| Garages | 1) Restoration and fabrication work goes uncredited once the car is resold or shown. 2) Proving workmanship to the next customer (word of mouth is the main marketing). 3) Customers asking for "a list of everything you did" years later. |
| Suppliers/brands | 1) No idea which builds their parts end up on after sale through distributors. 2) UK-built heritage brands (exhausts, suspension, restoration parts) want to be seen on finished cars. 3) Their brand name gets dropped when a car is photographed or sold. |

### How to frame FuelMyBuild (no claims we can't keep)
- "Your car's history file, online: every mod, every brand, every date, with photos, on one link."
- "When the insurer asks what's been done, it's already listed." Say that it is listed; never say it is accepted, approved or compliant.
- Do **not** say: MOT-exempt, insurer-approved, DVLA, "proves originality", "adds £X to value".

### Video concepts
1. **"The folder"**: a buyer at an NEC-style sale flicks through a battered history file and finds missing pages. Match cut from the folder to the FMB build page on a phone, where every entry is dated and has a photo. Collector audience.
2. **"Declared"**: a mate on the phone to his insurer: "What mods have you done?" He goes quiet. The other mate opens his build link and reads off coilovers, brand and date. Light comedy; the CTA is the share family ("Send this to the mate with undeclared mods"). Keep it light and don't give legal advice.
3. **"Whose welds are those?"**: a resto-shop owner sees a car he restored in 2019 at a meet, now with a new owner, and his name is still on the build page as the garage credit. Garage audience; uses a backdated log entry (live feature).

### Culture references
- Use: NEC Classic Motor Show (November), Goodwood (Festival of Speed / Revival), Caffeine & Machine, Sunday meets, Mk2 Golf, E30, Escort RS / Cosworth, MX-5 (Mk1), Defender, Mini, Land Rover Series, Japanese imports (R33/R34, Supra), yellow rear plates, right-hand drive.
- Avoid: street-racing or "car meet chaos" imagery (meets have a bad press and dispersal orders), anything that looks like hiding mods from insurers, US muscle-car defaults.

### Platform notes
IG and TikTok are both strong (TikTok reaches 45% of adults). Classic owners skew older and are on Facebook and Instagram. Use IG Reels for collectors and garages, and TikTok for the younger modified scene.

### Risks
- Anything that reads as advice on how to word an insurance declaration. Keep it to "it's all listed."
- Using an American voice or American car vocabulary costs credibility within the first 3 seconds.
- The 1.93m DVLA figure is unverified, so keep it off screen.

## 3. Germany (rank 2)

### Why it fits
- **Tuning market:** the aftermarket is worth more than **€2bn** in Germany, led by wheels, tyres and chassis parts (BBE/VDAT tuning study 2023, [Automechanika press release PDF](https://sneak-preview-automechanika.messefrankfurt.com/content/dam/messefrankfurt-redaktion/automechanika/2024/press/press-release/03-pm-ergebnisse-tuningstudie-2023.pdf)). Germany is historically the largest tuning market in the EU ([Autohaus on VDAT](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/autohandel/vdat-stabile-tuning-umsaetze-2725426)). Home to many parts brands (wheels, suspension, exhausts, ECU tuners) and the Essen Motor Show, with 200k+ visitors ([EMS press release](https://www.essen-motorshow.de/press/press-texts/detail-ems/over-200000-visitors-celebrate-the-entire-world-of-automobiles-2193)).
- **Classics:** 923,538 historic vehicles at the start of 2026, up 4% (KBA via [Autohaus](https://www.autohaus.de/nachrichten/autohersteller/zahl-der-oldtimer-erreicht-neuen-rekordwert-jede-menge-kulturgut-3775822)). Techno-Classica drew 188k visitors in 2024 ([Magneto](https://magnetomagazine.com/articles/final-35th-edition-of-germanys-techno-classica-essen-event-gets-underway-in-april-2025)).
- **Workshops:** 21,830 independent ("freie") workshop sites with turnover above €100k, plus 13,950 franchised sites (DAT-Report 2025, via [vision mobility](https://vision-mobility.de/news/dat-report-wo-autos-gewartet-und-repariert-werden-392680.html)).
- **Paperwork by law:** non-standard parts need a test certificate (ABE / Teilegutachten / ECE). Most then need a **Änderungsabnahme** by an examiner (§19 StVZO). Without a certificate, the car needs an individual approval (Einzelbetriebserlaubnis). Several modifications together can invalidate the individual reports, so the whole car is assessed again ([TÜV Rheinland](https://www.tuv.com/germany/de/%c3%a4nderungsabnahme.html), [TÜV SÜD FAQ](https://www.tuvsud.com/de-de/branchen/mobilitaet-und-automotive/tuning-eintragungen-und-aenderungsabnahmen/tuning-faq)). Gutachten often have to be carried in the car.
- **H-Kennzeichen** (historic plate): needs a §23 StVZO assessment of originality. Period modifications are accepted when they can be shown to be period ([TÜV Rheinland](https://www.tuv.com/germany/de/kfz-gutachten/oldtimer-h-kennzeichen.html), [Auto Zeitung](https://www.autozeitung.de/oldtimer-zulassung-kennzeichen-ratgeber-68101.html)). Only 54% of classics carry an H-plate (ADAC via Autohaus).
- **Social:** Instagram reaches 31.3m (44.4% of adults); TikTok reaches 21.8m adults (31.1%) ([DataReportal DE 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-germany)). Reach is lower than in the UK, so expect slower organic growth.

### Language and register
- German, written natively. Don't translate from English line by line. ElevenLabs multilingual v2 handles German; get a native speaker to check stress on compound words.
- **Use "du"**. The tuning and Schrauber scene on IG and TikTok uses du. Use the "Sie" form only in a direct B2B outreach DM to an established brand or a Meisterbetrieb, and in any written terms.
- Register: matter-of-fact, precise, and dry. Germans distrust hype more than anyone. Use concrete facts (part, brand, date, km) and keep superlatives out.
- Scene words: *Projekt, Aufbau, Umbau, Schrauber, schrauben, Karre/Kiste (affectionate), Fahrwerk, Felgen, tieferlegen, Leistungssteigerung, eingetragen, Eintragung, Gutachten, Teilegutachten, ABE, Abnahme, Prüfer, Werkstatt (not "Garage", which means a parking garage), Tuner, Oldie/Oldtimer, Youngtimer, H-Kennzeichen, scheckheftgepflegt (full service history: the trust word in every classified ad), lückenlos dokumentiert, Rechnungen, Ordner.*
- Units and formats: PS (not hp), km, €, 12.03.2019, decimal comma (1,8T).

### Top pain points
| Audience | Pain points |
|---|---|
| Collectors / owners | 1) A folder of Gutachten, Abnahmen and invoices that must be kept, and some of them carried in the car. 2) On resale, "lückenlos dokumentiert" decides the price, and a gap raises suspicion. 3) For an H-plate, showing that a modification is period-correct depends on records nobody kept. |
| Werkstätten / Tuner | 1) Their work disappears into a customer's folder, with no public portfolio. 2) Customers come back years later asking what exactly was fitted. 3) Getting credit when a car they built is shown at Essen or at meets. |
| Parts brands | 1) No visibility of which cars their parts end up on after sale through dealers. 2) Brands with an ABE or Gutachten want their name next to "eingetragen" builds, not anonymous ones. 3) User photos rarely name the brand. |

### How to frame FuelMyBuild
- "Dein Projekt, lückenlos dokumentiert: jedes Teil mit Marke, jeder Umbau mit Datum und Foto, alles unter einem Link." (Your project, documented without gaps: every part with its brand, every modification with a date and a photo, all on one link.)
- "Für den Ordner im Kofferraum gibt's jetzt einen Link." (The folder in the boot now has a link.)
- You **may** show a user photographing their Gutachten into a log entry, because it's just a photo.
- Do **not** say: TÜV-konform, TÜV-geprüft, eintragungsfähig, "ersetzt die Papiere" (replaces the papers), "hilft beim H-Kennzeichen" (helps with the H-plate), or anything implying legal validity. FMB is a record, not a document authorities recognise. Say so plainly if asked.

### Video concepts
1. **"Der Ordner"**: an owner sells a scheckheftgepflegt E30 and the buyer asks for the folder. Two folders: one bursting, coffee-stained and missing the 2017 coilover Gutachten; the other is a link. Match cut from a folder tab to a log-entry card. Collector audience, 45 s.
2. **"Wer hat's gebaut?"**: a Golf Mk2 at a meet gets admired. The owner opens the build page and the Werkstatt is credited by name, with dates. The shop owner in the background nods. Garage audience, 35 s.
3. **"Welche Felgen sind das?"**: comments under every car photo ask "Welche Felgen? Welches Fahrwerk?" The build link answers with brand, size, date and who fitted them. Supplier audience (the brand gets named on every car). CTA: "Schreib uns PARTS".

### Culture references
- Use: Essen Motor Show (late Nov to early Dec, timed with our test), Techno-Classica/Retro Classics, Nürburgring and Nordschleife touristenfahrten, VW Golf GTI generations, BMW E30/E36/E46, Audi 80/S2/RS2, Porsche 911 (964/993), Mercedes W123/W124 and the 190E 2.3-16, Opel Manta (affectionately), youngtimers. Licence-plate formats and Prüfplaketten (inspection stickers) as visual texture.
- Avoid: Wörthersee (that's Austria, and the scene has a "Poser" image), "Raser" (street racer) or "Autoposer" imagery (police crackdowns are a news theme), mod jokes about dodging the TÜV, US muscle cars as the default car, and "Garage" for a workshop.

### Platform notes
Instagram is stronger with adults (44% vs 31%). The classic and Werkstatt audience is on IG and Facebook groups; the younger tuning scene is on TikTok. Lead with IG Reels and cross-post to TikTok. YouTube long-form is big in the German scene and is out of scope for now, but Shorts is worth cross-posting.

### Risks
- **Biggest risk: an implied legal claim.** Any hint that FMB "counts" for the TÜV, the Zulassungsstelle or an H-plate assessment. Have a native speaker check every line against the claims list above.
- Translation pitfalls: "Build" is understood but can sound Denglisch; prefer "Projekt" or "Aufbau" in voiceover, though "Build-Page" is fine as a product term. "Garage" is wrong. "Credit" → "genannt / Credits gehen an …".
- Comment and DM replies in German are needed for the done-for-you CTA. Don't run that CTA until someone can answer in German.

## 4. Australia (rank 3)

### Why it fits
- **Modification is regulated by engineering:** modifications are measured against VSB 14 (the national code of practice for light-vehicle modifications), and each state certifies them in its own way ([VSB 14](https://infrastructure.gov.au/infrastructure-transport-vehicles/vehicles/vehicle-design-regulation/rvs/bulletins/ncop)). In Victoria, a VASS signatory issues an approval certificate and a **blue modification plate** ([Transport Victoria](https://transport.vic.gov.au/registration-and-licensing/registration/standard-and-non-standard-vehicle-information/modified-and-non-standard-vehicles/vass-approval-certificate)). In NSW, VSCCS or older ECS certificates apply, and owners are advised to carry their certificates because police can issue a **defect notice** that takes the car off the road until it's inspected ([Outback Travel Australia](https://outbacktravelaustralia.com.au/?p=17514), [WA defect notice](https://transport.wa.gov.au/licensing/vehicle/penalties-infringements/yellow-sticker)).
- **Aftermarket:** the AAAA says the industry contributes $58bn to the economy and has 50,000+ businesses and ~300,000 employees (2020–21 figures, [AAAA](https://aaaa.com.au/)). SUVs are 60.7% and light commercials 22.6% of sales, which drives demand for 4x4 accessories ([Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/australia-automotive-aftermarket-market), vendor estimate). There are many local 4x4 and performance brands.
- **Scene:** Summernats had 130k+ attendees, 2,800 entrants and A$46.6m of economic impact in 2025; 3,000 entrants were expected in 2026 ([Region Canberra](https://region.com.au/love-it-or-hate-it-summernats-is-an-undeniable-boon-to-the-act-economy/891701/), [Braidwood Times](https://www.braidwoodtimes.com.au/story/9147491/summernats-2026-revs-up-with-heat-and-horsepower)).
- **Collector market:** Holden Monaros at A$300k+, Torana A9X at A$230k. Auctioneers credit **originality and provenance** for record prices ([Retro Rides](https://retrorides.com.au/news/april-2025-auction-report/), [JustCars](https://www.justcars.com.au/news-and-reviews/aussie-classics-shine-at-shannons-melbourne-late-summer-auction/11547)). Holden is gone (closed 2020), so its heritage is finite and documented.
- **Social:** Instagram reaches 14.3m (**65.4% of adults**, the highest of any candidate); TikTok reaches 8.0m adults (38%) ([DataReportal AU 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-australia)).

### Language and register
- Australian English. Use an Australian voice. Register: relaxed, direct, matey, self-deprecating, allergic to wankery (pretension). Short and warm.
- Words: *mods, the build, engineered / engineering cert, mod plate (VIC), rego, defect / defected, roadworthy (RWC) / pink slip (NSW), ute, 4WD / 4x4, tourer, lift, bar work, Commodore, Falcon, Monaro, Torana, LandCruiser, Hilux, Patrol, mate, arvo, servo.* "Shop" is used, but "workshop" is better.
- Units: km, kW officially (enthusiasts also say hp), A$, 12/03/2019, right-hand drive.

### Top pain points
| Audience | Pain points |
|---|---|
| Owners/collectors | 1) Keeping engineering certificates and proof of mods with the car (being defected at a roadside stop is a real fear). 2) The rules differ by state, so moving interstate means paperwork again. 3) For Aussie classics, prices hinge on originality and provenance (matching numbers, build sheets). |
| Workshops (4x4 fitters, performance shops, resto shops) | 1) Customers ask "what did you fit and when?" for warranty or certification. 2) Their work on show cars (Summernats) goes uncredited after a sale. 3) Fitting many brands on one vehicle (bar, lift, drawers, lights) with no tidy record to hand over. |
| Suppliers (4x4 and performance brands) | 1) They don't know which vehicles their kit is on. 2) Brand credit is lost when tourers are sold second-hand. 3) Proving fitment combinations work (lift + tyres + bar) to the next buyer. |

### How to frame FuelMyBuild
- "Every mod on your rig, every brand, every date, on one link. Snap your engineering cert into the log while you're at it." A photo of a certificate is just a photo, which is fine.
- Do **not** say: "keeps you legal", "stops defects", "replaces your engineering cert", "roadworthy", or state-authority names as partners.

### Video concepts
1. **"Pulled over"**: a LandCruiser tourer at a roadside check. "What's been done to it?" The owner shows the build page on his phone: lift, brand, date, who fitted it, and a photo of the cert in the log entry. No outcome is claimed; it ends on "Mate, it's all there." 40 s.
2. **"The last Holden"**: an owner restoring a VL/VK Commodore or an HQ. A backdated log entry from 1998, from Dad's photos, then the story to now. Credit goes to the shop. Collector audience, 50–60 s, emotional (Holden is gone).
3. **"Whose bar is that?"**: at a 4x4 campsite, everyone asks about one bar or set of drawers. The build page names the brand and the fitter. Supplier audience; CTA "DM PARTS".

### Culture references
- Use: Summernats (January, Canberra), Bathurst 1000 (October), Street Machine magazine, 4x4 touring (Cape York, the Simpson), Holden vs Ford, Toyota LandCruiser 70/79 and 200/300, Hilux, Ranger, Nissan Patrol GU, Skylines and Silvias (imported), utes, burnout culture (with care).
- Avoid: hoon imagery (hoon laws mean car seizure in most states; burnout comps are fine only in a sanctioned arena), US muscle-car defaults, left-hand-drive interiors, A$ pricing errors.

### Platform notes
Instagram is the strongest platform (65% of adults); TikTok is decent (38%). The 4x4 scene is very IG- and YouTube-heavy. Lead with IG. Time zones: AEST/AEDT is 15–16 hours ahead of US Eastern, so schedule natively, not as a US re-post.

### Risks
- The state-by-state rules make any regulatory line easy to get wrong. Keep paperwork in the background ("the cert's in the log"), never the claim.
- Season: the test window (Nov) is early summer, which suits 4x4 trips, show season and Christmas-holiday driving. Never use snowy US footage.

## 5. Japan (rank 4): supplier, shop and export angle

### Why it fits
- **The parts and shop capital of tuning:** Tokyo Auto Salon 2026 drew ~270,000 visitors, with 385 companies and 840 custom cars ([Toyota Times](https://toyotatimes.jp/en/newscast/169.html), [TAS release](https://www.tokyoautosalon.jp/2026/media/en/release/1203_TAS2026_en.pdf)). Japanese car customisation is ~US$3.65bn (2025) and the aftermarket parts market ~US$30.7bn ([IMARC via GII](https://www.gii.co.jp/report/imarc1957011-japan-car-customization-market-size-share-trends.html), vendor estimates). Brands like HKS, Tein, RAYS, Cusco, Spoon and Nismo are the parts US/UK/AU builders buy.
- **Strict inspection:** a 2-yearly **shaken**. Illegal modifications get flagged ([Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motor-vehicle_inspection_(Japan))). Changes beyond tolerances (about ±3 cm length, ±2 cm width, ±4 cm height, ±50/100 kg) need a structural-change inspection (構造等変更検査) or a record change within 15 days ([MLIT notice](https://www.mlit.go.jp/notice/noticedata/pdf/20210804/jidousha1-1.pdf), [Office Tree](https://office-tree.jp/blog/vehicle/vehicle-structural-modification-approval/)). "車検対応" (shaken-compliant) and JASMA-certified exhausts are selling points.
- **Export provenance:** the US 25-year rule makes model-year 2000–2001 JDM cars importable now ([JDMBuySell guide](https://www.jdmbuysell.com/learn/topics/25-year-rule/)). Buyers abroad want history. Japanese shops and exporters can hand over a build link in English.
- **Social is different:** X reaches 70.9m (64.6% of adults), Instagram 57.5m (49.8%), TikTok only 26.9m adults (25.3%), and LINE has 97m users ([DataReportal JP 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-japan)).
- **Incumbent:** **みんカラ (Minkara)**, LY Corp's car SNS, where owners post 整備手帳 (maintenance logs) and parts reviews ([Minkara](https://minkara.carview.co.jp/), [LY Corp release](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/ja/news/release/017387/)). A domestic owner-to-owner build log already exists. We shouldn't fight it head-on.

### Language and register
- Japanese, **written by a native speaker**, not translated. ElevenLabs multilingual v2 speaks Japanese, but its intonation needs native QA.
- Register: polite です/ます in the voiceover (friendly polite, not keigo-stiff); brand and shop outreach in proper business Japanese. Avoid English-style hype and imperative CTAs ("Comment BUILD now!") — use soft invitations: 「プロフィールのリンクからどうぞ」 ("from the link in our profile").
- Words: *愛車 (aisha, "my beloved car", the key emotional word), カスタム, チューニング, 整備記録簿 / 記録簿付き (maintenance record book / "with records", a trust phrase in used-car ads), 車検対応, パーツ, ショップ, 施工 (installation by a shop), 取付日 (fitting date), 旧車 (classic), ネオクラ (neo-classic), JDM (used for the export audience), 25年ルール.*
- Formats: ¥, km, 馬力/PS, dates 2019年3月12日 or 2019/03/12, right-hand drive.

### Top pain points
| Audience | Pain points |
|---|---|
| Collectors/owners | 1) Shaken every 2 years and record changes for dimension changes, so they need to know exactly what was fitted. 2) 記録簿 (records) decide used-car value. 3) Selling to an export buyer who can't read Japanese paperwork. |
| Shops (チューニングショップ, 旧車 restorers) | 1) Builds leave for overseas and the shop's name gets lost. 2) Overseas buyers ask for history in English. 3) Showcasing work beyond the domestic Minkara and Instagram audience. |
| Parts brands | 1) Exported cars carry their parts abroad unattributed. 2) Grey-market and counterfeit confusion abroad, so a brand-named registry entry helps buyers see what's genuine (without claiming verification). 3) Reaching US/AU builders directly. |

### How to frame FuelMyBuild
- To shops and brands: "海外のオーナーにも、あなたの仕事が名前付きで残ります" (your work stays credited by name, even with owners overseas). An English build page that travels with the car.
- To owners: 愛車の記録を、パーツのブランドと日付ごとにひとつのリンクで (your car's record, by part brand and date, on one link).
- Do **not** say: 車検対応 (shaken-compliant), 公認 (officially approved), 証明 (proof/certification), 正規品保証 (genuine-part guarantee), or claim any MLIT or inspection relevance. Don't position as a Minkara replacement.

### Video concepts
1. **"25 years later"** (bilingual, for shops/exporters): an R34 or a JZA80 leaves a Yokohama port. Its Japanese shop's log entries (backdated, with photos) travel on one link to a new owner in the US. Voiceover in Japanese with an English on-screen headline. Supplier/garage audience.
2. **"Who fitted it?"**: a cut from a 2008 install at a Japanese shop to the car at a US meet in 2026. The shop's name is still on it.
3. **"記録簿"**: an owner flips a worn maintenance record book, then sees the same history with photos and the brand of every part. Collector audience; soft and polite.

### Culture references
- Use: Tokyo Auto Salon (January), Daikoku PA (careful: police close it on weekends; show it only as a place, not as a meet scene), Fuji Speedway, Tsukuba, kei cars and kei trucks, AE86, S-chassis, Skyline GT-R, Supra, NSX, RX-7, Civic Type R, Honda Beat, 旧車 like the Hakosuka.
