# Two-week sprint: 10 videos a day, Mon Oct 26 – Sun Nov 8 (W44–W45)

Why Oct 26 and not this Monday: 10 a day needs about 45 new reels plus the image bank and keys in place first. This week (Oct 19–25) is the ramp: the approved W42 queue keeps posting, the 9 lab reels are re-made as clean production masters, and the first production batches run.

## Volume
- **140 Instagram slots** (10 a day × 14). YouTube takes 6 of each day's 10, because the API quota is about 6 uploads a day (`publishing/references/platform-setup.md`). TikTok takes 5, because the unaudited app can only send 5 inbox drafts a day and Wayne taps Post on each.
- **Unique bodies: about 54.** Each body ships with 2–3 hooks, and the same body never airs twice within 5 days (days d, d+5, d+10 at most). That works out to an average of 2.6 posts per body.
  - 9 lab reels from W44 (A+, B, C v3), re-made to the new standard.
  - 45 new: lane 0 (Claude) 15, lane C (Claude's concept, ChatGPT critique, Claude rebuild) 10, lane A (ChatGPT blind) 10, lane B (ChatGPT informed) 10.
- **Spacing rules for the calendar:**
  - No audience three times in a row.
  - Never two lanes' takes on the same topic on the same day.
  - The CTA family rotates every post.
  - Lengths are mixed through the day: short ones in the morning slots, longer stories in the evening.

**Production pace:** about 41 new reels by Oct 28 means roughly 3–4 built a day from now. Claude's lanes (0 and C) start immediately. Lanes A and B need ChatGPT scripts by Oct 20–23. **Fallback if production slips:** relax the gap to 4 days, which lowers the opening need from 50 bodies to 40.

**CTA cap:** comment/DM keyword CTAs (BUILD, SHOP, PARTS) stay at no more than 1 in 4 slots until a reply method is chosen. The rest use link-in-bio and share/save. The script-producer applies this when it picks each reel's CTA, overriding the calendar's CTA column where needed.

The tts script already rotates keys and stops at ALL_KEYS_FAILED. One batch never needs more than 10k.

## Pipeline per reel (automated)
1. **Concept** from the sprint map (`plan/2wk-concepts.md`), checked against `state/visual-bank.md`.
2. **Script, 2–3 hooks, CTA family, length 30–60 s.**
   - Lanes 0/C: Claude.
   - Lanes A/B: ChatGPT via the OpenAI API, if a key is set; otherwise Wayne pastes.
   - Lane C: ChatGPT critiques, then Claude rewrites.
3. **Retention edit**, then **VO** (ElevenLabs batch).
4. **Build:** the motion-director on Opus, using the image bank. No captions, no labels, always alive, story-carrying transitions.
5. **Automatic QA:** frame checks, motion energy, loudness, claims vs live features, uniqueness. Then the queue.
6. **Posting:** Wayne approves each week's calendar once (studio rule), then the posting routine runs it.
7. **Sunday review:** metrics per lane, hook winners, drop seconds, then the next week's fixes. This uses the existing weekly routine, updated for the sprint.

## Decisions (Wayne, 2026-10-15)
- **Comment/DM keyword replies:** Wayne answers by hand for now. Keyword CTAs stay at about 1 in 4 to keep his reply load manageable.
- **YouTube:** paused until Wayne finishes its setup. Don't upload or schedule YouTube items. Keep `yt_*` fields in the queue so it can resume.
- **Instagram:** 10 a day, automatic.
- **TikTok:** 10 a day in two waves of 5 inbox drafts.
  - The first run of the day sends wave 1 (slots 1–5) and the 14:45 ET run sends wave 2 (slots 6–10).
  - If TikTok refuses wave 2 because drafts are still pending, the run retries on the next run and tells Wayne.
  - Wayne posts each draft, pasting its caption from the TikTok caption board.
- **TikTok caption board:** one page Wayne can open on his phone, republished at the same link every day.
  - Each draft has its own card, in send order: wave and draft number, post time (ET and Nairobi), the video title, its opening line or first on-screen words, a thumbnail of the first frame, and the caption with a Copy button. That way he knows which caption belongs to which draft.
  - The push notification links to the board.
- **Network:** `api.openai.com` and `api.elevenlabs.io` are already reachable from the environment. Only the keys are missing.
