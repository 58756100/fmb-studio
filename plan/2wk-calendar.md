# Two-week calendar: Mon Oct 26 – Sun Nov 8, 2026 (140 Instagram slots), revision 2

Companion to `plan/2wk-concepts.md` (concept details), `plan/2wk-sprint-2026-10-26.md` (volume, caps, decisions of Oct 15, 16 and 17) and `plan/intl-research.md`.

Revision 2 applies Wayne's Oct 17 decisions: lane weighting, S0-04/07/10/12/13 moved to lane C, voices and pace as columns, YouTube paused, TikTok in two waves, and 7 reserved international slots.

## How to read it
- Times are ET (clocks go back on Sun Nov 1; times stay in local ET). Ten slots a day: 08:00, 09:30, 11:00, 12:30, 14:00, 15:30, 17:00, 18:30, 20:00, 22:00. The posting routine needs these ten slots added.
- Hook: A, B, C = the hook variants of one body, in airing order (first airing A, second B, third C). A body that airs once is hook A only; twice, A and B.
- Lane: `lab` = the 9 lab reels, `0` = Claude alone, `C` = Claude concept, ChatGPT critique, Claude rebuild (15 bodies), `A` = ChatGPT blind, `B` = ChatGPT informed, `INTL` = reserved international slot, no body yet.
- Aud: COL collectors, GAR garages, SUP suppliers, ALL everyone.
- Len: seconds. Lanes 0 and C use the written target length. Lanes A and B use the length in the ChatGPT file. Lab lengths are provisional (the masters get re-timed).
- CTA codes: DFY = done-for-you (comment BUILD). LNK = link in bio / fuelmybuild.com. SHR = share/save. SHP = DM "SHOP". PRT = DM "PARTS". DFY, SHP and PRT are the keyword CTAs. A body keeps one CTA on all its airings (the writers' rule: one CTA per master).
- Pace: `fast` or `normal`. Decided by slot: slots 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 of each day (09:30, 12:30, 15:30, 18:30, 22:00) are fast, slots 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 are normal. Each body sits in the same slot on all its airings, so its pace never changes, and pace alternates all day and across midnight.
- Voice: `Jerry B.` (premium luxury commercial), `Emma` (professional commercial), `Justin Case` (trustworthy ad narrator), `two-voice` (dialogue or interview reels), `audition` (character voice not on the roster). One voice per body, the lead voice. Where a script has a second speaker or a separate CTA voice, that one is set at casting.
- TT: TikTok wave. W1 = slots 1–5 (first run of the day), W2 = slots 6–10 (14:45 ET run). Every IG slot also goes to a TikTok draft. YouTube is paused: no flags, no uploads.
- `*` after a body marks a note in the "Notes on specific rows" section.
