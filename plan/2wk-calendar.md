# Two-week calendar: Mon Oct 26 – Sun Nov 8, 2026 (140 Instagram slots)

Companion to `plan/2wk-concepts.md` (concept details) and `plan/2wk-sprint-2026-10-26.md` (volume, caps, batches).

## How to read it
- Times are ET (clocks go back on Sun Nov 1; times stay in local ET). Ten slots a day: 08:00, 09:30, 11:00, 12:30, 14:00, 15:30, 17:00, 18:30, 20:00, 22:00. The posting routine needs these ten slots added (the plan's list of 08/10/13/15/17/20/22 plus the existing four runs).
- Hook: A, B, C = the three hook variants of one body. First airing is A, second B, third C. Four late bodies (LB-03, LB-06, LB-09, LB-10) air once, so hook A only.
- Lane: `lab` = the 9 lab reels, `0` = Claude, `C` = Claude concept with ChatGPT critique, `A` = ChatGPT blind, `B` = ChatGPT informed.
- Aud: COL collectors, GAR garages, SUP suppliers, ALL everyone.
- Len: target seconds. Lab lengths and all A/B lengths are provisional (ChatGPT sets A/B lengths; the lab masters get re-timed). The row is a planning estimate.
- CTA codes: DFY = done-for-you (comment BUILD, we set up the build page). LNK = link in bio / fuelmybuild.com. SHR = share/save. SHP = DM "SHOP", we load your first builds. PRT = DM "PARTS", we show you where your parts end up (no supplier-page promise). The pattern is DFY or audience, LNK, share or audience, LNK, repeating, so LNK is 50% of slots and comment/DM keywords stay about 1 in 4 to 1 in 2 until a reply tool is chosen (plan blocker 3). SHP and PRT also need DM replies.
- Platforms: Y = also goes to YouTube Shorts (exactly 6 each day), T = also goes to a TikTok draft (exactly 5 each day). IG always.
- Hook families: each concept lists its A/B/C families in the concepts file. At queue time, pick the family order per slot so neighbouring slots never share a family (not checkable for lane A/B until ChatGPT returns hooks).

## Rule checks
- 140 rows, 10 a day; exactly 6 Y and 5 T each day.
- Every body airs 1 to 3 times, always exactly 5 days apart.
- No audience appears three times in a row, including across midnight.
- The CTA family changes every slot, including across days.
- Same-topic pairs sit in different groups, so they never share a day: lab A and lab B twins (A-L1/B-L1, A-L2/B-L2, A-L3/B-L3), S0-11 vs LA-05, S0-13 vs LB-09, S0-12 vs LB-07, S0-02 vs LA-03, S0-07 vs LA-04.
- Lengths run short to long through each day.
